**Navigate your next.**
Prepared for digital advertising and campaign work. Built from the supplied Infosys brand style guide and real assets (logos, fonts, colour values), and verified against current public brand facts (tagline and heritage line active as of 2026; NYSE: INFY).

This document is the **hub**. Every font, logo, colour and downstream asset referenced here lives in the four-folder package described in §0.1.

## 0. Masterbrand architecture (read first)

Infosys is a **masterbrand / house of brands**, so precedence rules govern everything below.

- **Infosys is always the masterbrand** and is never subordinate or equal to any other mark, including an acquired brand or a partner.
- **Endorsed product brands** — EdgeVerve (with the “An Infosys company” byline) and Finacle — carry their own visual equities. **Their own product guidelines govern product work.** These digital guidelines govern Infosys masterbrand advertising.
- **Branded offerings** (e.g. an “Information App”) sit under the Infosys wordmark, separated by a rule the height of the “I”, and must be expressed in **26 characters or less**.
- **Partner co-branding** (e.g. Infosys + Oracle): logos appear equal in size and weight, Infosys upper-left, partner upper-right, and Infosys appears **without the tagline**.
- **Precedence for agents and designers:** a product-brand asset (Finacle, EdgeVerve) must follow that product's guidelines; never apply a masterbrand device to a product-brand logo, and never apply Finacle Red to the Infosys masterbrand.

## 1. Brand overview

**One-line positioning.** Infosys is a global digital transformation and consulting leader that helps organisations navigate their next opportunity through AI, innovation and continuous learning.

**Core purpose.** To amplify human potential and create the next opportunity for people, businesses and communities.

**Brand idea — "Navigate your next."** We navigate our clients from where they are to where they aspire to be. Every ad should leave the feeling of **confident forward movement** — optimism grounded in expertise.

**Heritage line.** "Powered by Intellect. Driven by Values." Used mainly for "who we are" / internal expression, not as the advertising tagline.

**Three brand pillars** (the proof behind the promise):

| Pillar | What it means in an ad |
|---|---|
| AI-powered core | Modernise and intelligence-enable the systems that run the enterprise |
| Agile digital at scale | Respond and change at the speed of the market |
| Always-on learning | Continuously re-skill people and the enterprise |

**Brand values.** Client value · Leadership by example · Integrity & transparency · Fairness · Excellence.

Every recommendation below traces to this brief: category (digital services, consulting & technology), audience (global enterprises and their leaders), promise ("navigate your next"), five personality adjectives (**confident, global, thoughtful, vibrant, forward-focused**), and the core feeling every ad must leave (**confident forward movement**).

## 2. Personality & voice

**Voice principle:** *a voice of quiet confidence — heard as much in how we listen as in what we say.*

**Personality adjectives:** confident but humble · global and inclusive · professional and knowledgeable · clear and concise · thoughtful and respectful · forward-focused.

**Tone in digital advertising.**
- Lead with the **client's next**, not with Infosys' accomplishments. ("Your next digital discovery will solve the unsolvable" — not "Infosys builds AI.")
- Speak efficiently. Short, declarative, active sentences — each new thought building on the last.
- Use a Western, American-English default (be pragmatic for local trade press). Avoid idioms, jargon, acronyms (spell out on first use), hyperbole and superlatives. Use facts, not boasts.
- Infuse the idea of **"be more"** without necessarily using the words.
- Never touch gender, race, ethnicity, politics or religion in creative.

**Signature-line discipline.** The public tagline is exactly **"Navigate your next"** (sentence case in the lockup). Do not paraphrase it, translate it in public work, or bolt a prefix/suffix onto it.

**Do / don't messaging.**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Your next data centre will be integrated." | "Infosys is the world's best at data centres." |
| "Modernize the core before you make your next move." | "Revolutionary, game-changing AI!!!" |
| Client outcome first, quiet confidence | Breathless, solicitous, hyperbolic |

## 3. Visual identity system

### 3.1 Logo

The logo is our visual promise; nothing we produce appears without it. Use the **real supplied master artworks** — never recreate, re-trace, re-colour or re-proportion them. See `Logo-Reference.html`.

**Official master artwork.**

| Use | File | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| Primary, on light | `Infosys_WB.svg` | Light / white |
| Primary, reversed | `Infosys_DB.svg` (white) | Dark / medium |
| Tagline lockup, light | `Infosys-tagline_WB.svg` | Light |
| Tagline lockup, reversed | `Infosys-tagline_DB.svg` | Dark / medium |
| Branded offering, light | `Infosys_offering_WB.svg` | Light |
| Branded offering, reversed | `Infosys_offering_DB.svg` | Dark / medium |

**Colourway decision rule.** The logo colour depends on the background *directly behind the logo*: Infosys Blue (or Chrome / 70% black) on light grounds; **white in reverse** on dark, medium or photographic grounds. Reverse out of a photo only when the area behind the logo is graphically simple and unobtrusive.

**Clear space:** a margin equal to the height of the "I" (x) on all sides — free of type, images and other logos.
**Minimum digital size:** **90 px wide** (print minimum 0.75 in / 19.05 mm). Below 90 px the wordmark is not legible.

**Permitted logo colours (only these):** Infosys Blue `#007CC3`, Infosys Chrome `#6D6E71`, 70% black, or white in reverse.

**Misuse (never):** rotate/skew/3D/outline the logo · change its typeface or proportions · add shadows or effects · recolour it or use a non-brand colour · place the blue logo on a blue ground · add a prefix, suffix or hybrid mark · use it inside body text · translate its characters.

> **Colour flag — three blues in the supplied artwork.** `Infosys_WB.svg` is filled **#0072AD**, the tagline lockup is **#007CC3** (the stated Infosys Blue), and the offering lockup is **#007DC3**. **Standardise digital on #007CC3** and re-export the plain logo to match before production. (Recorded in `infosys-colours.json → logo_blue_discrepancy`.)

### 3.2 Navigation Portal graphic (signature device)

The Navigation Portal is an extension of the logo — it repeats the "I" to make **Portals**, with one larger **Activation Portal** at the centre.

- **Orientation:** always horizontal, never rotated, skewed or angled.
- **Proportion:** never change the ratio of Portals to Activation Portal; never make Portals grow toward the centre; never overlap or make irregular patterns.
- **Colour:** on white it is medium + light Sapphire; on a coloured ground the Portals are the *light* set colour and the Activation Portal the *medium* set colour **from the same colour set**. The Portal is never used plain (without an image or design element inside/around the Activation Portal).
- **Activation Portal uses:** hold photography (light backgrounds), an illustration (white portal on medium grounds), a headline (headline type only, centred between Portals), or the logo (centred, "I" height = one Portal).

In digital, the Portal is the recurring motif that signals "Infosys" at a glance — carry it into banners, stories and video as vertical bars framing the message.

### 3.3 Colour

Full values, colour sets and the WCAG table are in `Color-Reference.html` / `infosys-colours.json`.

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB | Pantone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary (cornerstone) | Infosys Blue | `#007CC3` | 0 124 195 | 285 C |
| Primary (stabiliser) | Infosys Chrome | `#6D6E71` | 109 110 113 | Cool Gray 11 C |

- **Screening (tints) of the primary colours is not permitted.**
- Six colour sets extend the palette — **Sapphire** (primary), Coral, Topaz, Jade, Amethyst, Onyx — each with Ultra Light / Light / Medium / Dark. **Never mix two sets in one asset. Never use gradients.**
- Set-level usage: Ultra Light = backgrounds/illustrations · Light = Portals/illustrations · Medium = Activation Portal, backgrounds, call-out text · Dark = typography, line art, logos.
- **Finacle Red `#ED1C2E` is restricted to Infosys Finacle only.**

**WCAG for digital (measured, AA floor):** white on Infosys Blue = 4.5:1 (AA) · Infosys Blue on white = 4.5:1 (AA) · Chrome on white = 5.1:1 (AA) · white on Sapphire Dark = 17.6:1 (AAA). **Watch-outs:** white type **fails** on Jade Medium (2.7:1) and Topaz Medium (2.41:1) and only passes on Coral Medium at large sizes (3.0:1). On those grounds use Sapphire Dark or Black type, or reserve white for large display headlines only.

### 3.4 Typography

Three families; full specimen in `Type-Reference.html`.

| Role | Family | Files | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlines | **Tungsten Medium** (+ SemiBold) | `Tungsten-Medium.ttf`, `Tungsten-SemiBold.ttf` | Condensed, vertical headlines that echo the "I". Headlines only — not body. |
| Body | **Myriad Pro** (9 weights) | `MYRIADPRO-REGULAR/SEMIBOLD/BOLD/COND/BOLDCOND/…OTF` | Print and web body, subheads, captions. Bold Condensed for online banner headlines/links. |
| Presentation & web fallback | **Arial** | system | PowerPoint, HTML email, general web copy when Tungsten/Myriad can't embed. |

**Digital type scale (mobile minimum 14px):** Display 48–96px Tungsten Medium (uppercase) · H2 28–40px Tungsten SemiBold · Subhead 20–24px Myriad Pro Semibold · Body 16–18px Myriad Pro Regular, line-height ≥1.5 · Caption/legal 12–14px Myriad Pro Cond.

**Web CSS stacks** — headline: `"Tungsten Medium","Arial Narrow",sans-serif` · body: `"Myriad Pro",Arial,sans-serif`.

**Licensing (mandatory).** Tungsten is licensed from typography.com; Myriad Pro is an Adobe licensed font. External agencies must license their own copies. Sharing font files breaches the licence.

### 3.5 Imagery & art direction

Use photography with **depth and forward movement** (perspective corridors, motion), crisp lighting, bright colours, subjects tied to the services shown, content close to brand colours. Use **silhouette** photography (subject knocked out on a set colour) to emphasise a moment. **Do not** use staged, busy, generic, dark/uneven, conceptual still-life, dramatic, deserted, abstract, blurry, filtered, overlaid or vector images. Represent people with dignity and inclusive casting.

> Licensed photography and approved client imagery are supplied by the brand team. In layouts and campaigns below, `[image zone]` marks exactly where they drop in — no photography is fabricated here.

### 3.6 Iconography

Custom line icons: perfect circles/edges, 6px thin line, rounded corners (R 0.35in scale), 65% arrowheads, **single medium brand colour**. Never multi-colour, hand-drawn, filled, shadowed/3D, or in a sequence of three orange dots. The three pillar icons (AI-powered core, Agile digital at scale, Always-on learning) are the core set — obtain approved icons from the brand team.

### 3.7 Motion & sonic

Prescribed logo animation: vertical bars ("I" strokes) assemble left-to-right into the wordmark, then the tagline resolves — mirroring the Portal build. In digital motion, keep transitions horizontal and forward-moving; the Activation Portal is the focal frame. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: provide a static first/last frame. (No approved sonic logo is defined in the supplied guide.)

## 5. Messaging & copywriting

**Message pillars** map to the three brand pillars (§1). Structure copy in an inverted pyramid: most important first.

**Language patterns that work (from the brand's own creative):**
- "Your next [X] will [outcome]." — e.g. "Your next data centre will be integrated."
- "Modernize / navigate [the core / your next] …"
- Close with the signature: "The journey to digital has so many nexts. Navigate your next with Infosys." + `infosys.com`.

**Emotional triggers:** confidence, optimism, momentum, capability — never fear, shame or hype.

**Localisation / locales.** Default **en (American English)** for global work; adapt to British English for UK-only trade press. Convert monetary values to USD billions for global consumption; use "percent" not "%" in body text. Never machine-translate public work; translate meaning, not words; never mix locales in one asset (the endline "Navigate your next" may remain untranslated as a deliberate endline). Set `<html lang>` and `hreflang` correctly on web.

## 7. Campaign system — digital advertising layer

Portfolio split (guide): ~60% always-on performance/awareness in the core ad sizes, ~30% campaign bursts (hero video + social), ~10% experimental. Components: Navigation Portal frame, Activation Portal message, tagline lockup, pillar icons, `[image zone]`.

### 7.1 Exact platform specs

| Placement | Size (px) | Logo min | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram / Meta feed square | 1080 × 1080 | 90px wide | Safe area: keep type ≥64px from edges |
| Stories / Reels / vertical | 1080 × 1920 | 90px wide | Keep content out of top 250px & bottom 300px UI zones |
| Medium rectangle (MPU) | 300 × 250 | symbol-only if <120px tall | Workhorse banner |
| Leaderboard | 728 × 90 | 90px wide | Horizontal Portal band |
| Wide skyscraper | 160 × 600 | symbol/stacked | Vertical Portal column |
| Large rectangle | 336 × 280 | 90px | — |
| Mobile leaderboard | 320 × 50 | drop wordmark, symbol only | Tiny — headline + symbol only |
| Half-page | 300 × 600 | 90px | Room for pillar icons |
| Billboard | 970 × 250 | 90px | Hero horizontal |
| Square post (LinkedIn) | 1080 × 1080 | 90px | Primary B2B channel |
| Story hero video | 1080 × 1920 | 90px | ≤15s, captioned |
| Landscape video | 1280 × 720 | 90px | ≤30s, captioned |

### 7.2 Layout & safe-margin rules

- **Margins:** minimum padding = 4% of the shortest edge, or 24 / 32 / 40px min for small / medium / large units.
- **Grid:** logo upper-left (or upper-right when paired with a partner); message in/around the Activation Portal; tagline lockup + URL as the sign-off.
- **Colourway rule for the logo** follows the ground directly behind it (§3.1).
- **Minimum-size behaviour:** on 320×50 and 160×600, drop the wordmark and use the symbol alone; never shrink the wordmark below 90px.

### 7.3 Motion guidelines

- Horizontal, forward-moving transitions; Portal-build for reveals; Activation Portal is the focal frame.
- Hold the final logo + tagline lockup ≥1s (or 2s on 6s bumpers).
- Caption all video; provide a static fallback frame; respect reduced-motion.

### 7.4 Accessibility (non-negotiable)

WCAG **AA floor** (AAA for critical flows). Body ≥14px on mobile; contrast ≥4.5:1 normal / 3:1 large; never colour-alone for meaning; captions on video; visible focus states on interactive units; honour reduced-motion. Apply the §3.3 watch-outs on Jade/Topaz/Coral grounds.

### 7.5 Sample campaign — see `infosys_sample_campaign.html`

Campaign idea: **"Your next, now."** Tagline: **Navigate your next.** Executed across 1080×1080, 1080×1920, 970×250, 728×90, 300×250, 300×600, 160×600, 336×280, 320×50 and 1280×720, with a right/wrong example — full spec and rendered set in `infosys_sample_campaign.html` and template rules in `infosys_banner_guidelines.md`.

**Logo standard for campaigns:** every advertising unit uses the **Infosys logo-with-tagline lockup only** (`Infosys-tagline_WB.svg` / `Infosys-tagline_DB.svg`), colourway matched to its ground — so the tagline is the single, consistent signature and is not repeated in a separate sign-off. (Partner co-brand units are the only exception: Infosys appears without the tagline.)

**Executive thought-leadership (Template E).** The set includes the supplied **Salil Parekh** LinkedIn ad — "AI adoption strengthens demand for skilled technology talent" — as the reference for quote-led thought-leadership. Hard rules: the portrait must be an **approved, real photograph** of the named executive (never generated or recreated), and the quote must be an **approved executive statement** — exact wording, name and title signed off by the brand team and legal (§10). Never fabricate or paraphrase an executive quote.

## 9. Global vs local

**Locked (never localise):** logo and lockups, tagline "Navigate your next", colour system, type families, Navigation Portal construction, masterbrand precedence.

## 10. Compliance & brand safety (load-bearing)

The load-bearing concern for Infosys advertising is **claims + masterbrand precedence + client-result permission**. These are hard rules.

**Claims substantiation.**
- No unqualified superlatives or performance claims ("best," "#1," "fastest," "guaranteed ROI").
- Every capability, metric, market-position or AI claim must be **substantiated and approved** before use. Represent AI capabilities honestly — no implied autonomy or outcomes the product doesn't deliver.
- Statistics, awards and analyst positions require a dated, approved source.

**Client results & references.**
- Named clients, logos, quotes, metrics and case studies require **documented client permission** and legal sign-off. Lead with the client's achievement; never expose confidential client data.

**Masterbrand precedence & IP.**
- Infosys is never subordinate/equal to another mark; partner logos equal weight, Infosys without tagline in co-brand.
- **Finacle Red only for Finacle**; never apply product devices to the masterbrand or vice-versa.
- Respect exact trademark casing and third-party marks; do not imply a third party's product is from Infosys.

**People, dignity & safety.**
- Inclusive, dignified representation; no fear/shame; avoid gender, race, ethnicity, politics, religion in creative; special care with vulnerable audiences.

**Accessibility.** WCAG AA floor is a compliance item, not a nicety (§7.4).

**Approvals.** Brand team approves logo/Portal usage and imagery; legal approves claims, client references, prices and offer terms. Non-overridable rules are encoded for agents in §11.
