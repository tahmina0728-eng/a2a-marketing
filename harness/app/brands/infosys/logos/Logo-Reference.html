Infosys · Navigate your next · Logo reference

The logo is our visual promise

Our logo is the ultimate expression of the brand — nothing we produce should appear without it. Every mark on this page is the master artwork supplied for this project, shown on its correct ground, including the Topaz, Cobalt and Aster sub-brand lockups. Never recreate, re-trace, re-colour or re-proportion them.

On a light background the logo is Infosys Blue (or Infosys Chrome / 70% black in greyscale). On a dark or medium background it reverses to white. Readability must be maintained on every background.

03 — Sub-brand lockups

Cloud · Cobalt_WB.png

Marketing · Aster_WB.png

Sub-brands are stacked, two-tone lockups, not offering lockups: the Infosys wordmark sits above the sub-brand name, optically right-aligned to it, with no dividing rule. The wordmark is drawn at an identical size in all three files — 1,689 px wide in the supplied masters — so the sub-brand name, not the wordmark, sets the overall width. The wordmark is #007DC3; the sub-brand name is #004A85, set lowercase in the same typeface. The two elements are one artwork — never re-space, re-stack or set them side by side.

04 — Clear space & minimum size

Clear space (dashed) = height of the “I” (x). x must never be < 10 px online / 3 mm print.

90 px
min digital width

0.75 in / 19.05 mm
min print width

Minimum digital width: 90 px. Below this the wordmark loses legibility. Keep the surrounding clear space free of type, images and other logos.

Sub-brand minimum: to be confirmed. In a stacked lockup the Infosys wordmark occupies roughly half the artwork width, so a 90 px lockup renders the wordmark at about 45 px — under the primary minimum. Ask the brand team for the published sub-brand minimum rather than scaling to 90 px by analogy.

05 — Do not

Don’t rotate or set vertical
Don’t place blue logo on blue
Don’t recolour, outline or add effects
Don’t change the typeface / proportions
Don’t add a prefix, suffix or hybrid mark
Don’t split a sub-brand from the wordmark

Only three logo colours are ever permitted on the primary mark: Infosys Blue #007CC3, Infosys Chrome #6D6E71, or 70% black (plus white in reverse). Sub-brand lockups add #004A85 for the sub-brand name only — it is never used on the Infosys wordmark. Reverse out of a photo only when the image behind the logo is simple and unobtrusive.

06 — Colour audit

Four blues are baked into the supplied artwork. The plain logo Infosys_WB is filled #0072AD; the tagline lockup is #007CC3 (the stated Infosys Blue); the offering lockup and every sub-brand wordmark are #007DC3. Standardise digital on #007CC3 and re-export the plain logo and the sub-brand wordmarks to match before campaign production. Separately, confirm #004A85 is the approved sub-brand navy — it is not in the three-colour logo palette.
