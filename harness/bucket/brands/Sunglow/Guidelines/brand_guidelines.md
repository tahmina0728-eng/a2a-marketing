# Sunglow Brand Guidelines

**Enterprise Brand & Campaign System**
*Version 1.0 — Built for marketing teams, agencies, and AI-driven campaign generation systems.*

> **Brand context.** Sunglow is a hair care brand built for Black hair. We make shampoos, conditioners, and treatments that nourish, repair, and protect — and we celebrate the radiance of every type of Black hair, on its own terms.

> **Document purpose.** A single source of truth so any team — internal, agency, or automated — can produce on-brand work without additional guidance.

---

## 1. Brand Overview

### 1.1 Brand Promise
**Helping every kind of Black hair glow on its own terms.**

This promise is the test every campaign, pack design, and piece of copy must pass: does this help Black hair feel cared for, celebrated, and seen as it is?

### 1.2 Mission & Vision
- **Mission:** To make hair care that's designed for Black hair from the start — never adapted, never an afterthought — so every wearer can show up with their hair on its best day.
- **Vision:** A category where Black hair isn't a "specialty" aisle but the room itself.

### 1.3 Values
- **Made For** — products formulated for Black hair as the starting point, not a checkbox.
- **Radiant** — we celebrate shine, health, and visibility. Hair is meant to be seen.
- **Generous** — knowledge, care routines, and confidence shared freely.
- **Plural** — every texture, every length, every styling choice is welcome here.
- **Honest** — we say what's in the bottle and what it does. No miracle language.

### 1.4 Brand Story (positioning narrative)
For decades, hair care for Black hair has been the second formula off the line — a tweak to a product designed for someone else. Sunglow exists because Black hair deserves better than an afterthought. Our science starts with coily, kinky, curly, locked, braided, relaxed, transitioning, and protective-styled hair. Our shelves do too. Our promise is the simplest one in beauty: hair care made *for* you, not adjusted *for* you.

### 1.5 Core Brand Promise (Operational)
The promise broken into three commitments:
1. **Real care** — formulations built around Black hair's actual needs (moisture, definition, scalp health, breakage protection).
2. **Real visibility** — Black hair as the centre of every image, claim, and conversation.
3. **Real glow** — radiance you can see, feel, and trust — substantiated, not stylised.

### 1.6 Global vs Local Positioning
Sunglow operates as a **globally consistent masterbrand with deep regional and diasporic expression.** The identity, voice, and visual system stay constant; the casting, hair stories, languages, and cultural references adapt to the Black communities of each market we serve. See Section 9.

## 2. Brand Personality & Voice

### 2.1 Brand Archetype
**Caregiver** with a strong **Lover** influence. We nourish (we care for hair) and we celebrate (we love what we care for). Both come through warmth, expertise, and confident affection — never authority, never rescue narratives.

### 2.2 Personality Attributes
- **Warm**
- **Confident**
- **Knowing** (expertise lived, not claimed)
- **Affirming**
- **Plural** (we hold the whole range of Black hair)

### 2.3 Tone of Voice
Consistent across pack, social, advice content, ads:
- **Encouraging** — we hand confidence over.
- **Specific** — we name textures, products, results clearly.
- **Plain-spoken** — clear, real-world language. No clinical jargon, no salon-speak.
- **Quietly celebratory** — joy without theatre.

### 2.4 Voice Principle
**Talk like the friend who knows your hair.**

Not the hairstylist on a pedestal, not the dermatologist, not the brand. The friend who's been on the journey with you, who knows your texture, who texts "have you tried the leave-in?" Every line should pass the friend-who-knows-your-hair test.

### 2.5 Creative Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Hair is the hero** | The hair, not the bottle, leads every image. Pack supports, never dominates. Show the result, the texture, the movement, the light. |
| **Glow you can see** | We show, we don't tell. Shine, definition, bounce, edge — visible results. No "radiance" superlatives that the image can't back up. |
| **The room, not the aisle** | We design as if Black hair were the centre of beauty, not a specialty section. Every asset should feel like the main aisle. |

### 2.6 Hair Truths
We organise creative around small, real-life truths from Black hair journeys. A great Hair Truth is:
- **Lived** — a thing that actually happens (wash day, the satin bonnet, the moment the curls pop, the edges laid).
- **Shared** — recognisable across textures and styling choices.
- **Resolved** — Sunglow earns its place in the truth, doesn't dominate it.

Examples:
- "Wash day is a whole day, and that's okay."
- "The moment the conditioner hits and the curls start to talk back."
- "The first time you stopped fighting your hair."
- "When the bonnet earns its place on the bedside table."

### 2.7 Do's and Don'ts of Communication

**DO**
- Lead with the hair, the result, the lived moment.
- Name textures, types, and styles with specificity (4c, locs, twist-out, silk press, bantu knots, transitioning).
- Talk like the relationship is mutual — we know hair; you know yours.
- Earn humour and warmth from specificity, not generality.
- Celebrate. Joy is the default, not the bonus.

**DON'T**
- Use rescue or fixing language ("tame," "control," "manageable," "smooth out").
- Frame Black hair as a problem to solve.
- Use saviour or transformation tropes ("from this to this").
- Lean on "magic," "miracle," or vague "radiance" claims that aren't substantiated.
- Speak about Black hair from the outside — as if it's the exception.
- Use the wordmark or Circle without intentional colour and care.

### 2.8 Example Messaging
- "Made for the way your hair grows."
- "Shine that earns its name."
- "Wash day, easier. Glow, louder."
- "Your texture, on its best day."
- "The leave-in that finally listens."
- **Tagline:** "Let it glow." — used as a sign-off, not a workhorse line.

## 3. Visual Identity System

### 3.1 Logo — The Sunglow Circle

The Sunglow identity is a white wordmark inside a coloured circle — a soft, rounded disc with a subtle inner dimension and a darker outline at the edge.

#### The Three Construction Rules (non-negotiable)
1. **Always a perfect circle.** No ovals, no rounded squares, no soft asymmetry.
2. **Always white wordmark, centred and optically balanced** inside the circle.
3. **Always a darker outline (1–2% darker than the fill)** at the circle's edge — the soft dimensional cue is part of the identity.

#### Naming
Refer to the brand mark as **"the Sunglow Circle"** (the disc) or **"the Sunglow wordmark"** (the white type). Never abbreviate to "the logo" in formal documentation.

#### Clear Space
- Clearspace equals **25% of the Circle's diameter**, applied on all four sides outside the disc.
- Minimum size: **Print 10 mm diameter** / **Digital 40 px diameter**.
- At smaller sizes the wordmark loses legibility — use the Circle alone (no wordmark) as a brand dot instead.

#### Trademark
The mark requires ® on packaging, advertising, and corporate communications. Not required on internal-only or social organic where space is constrained.

#### Approval
Treated or non-standard uses (limited-edition colours, partnership co-locks, motion treatments, mark-as-pattern uses) require approval from **Global Brand + Legal IP** at concept and pre-final stages.

#### Permitted Techniques
- **Scale variation** — the Circle can flex large or small within minimum-size rules.
- **Colour variation** — see Section 3.4. The colour of the Circle is part of the system, not a fixed value.
- **Repetition** — the Circle can be repeated as a pattern element on packaging, secondary surfaces, and motion stings.
- **Cropping** — large-scale crops where the Circle exceeds the canvas (OOH, full-bleed digital). Crops must never obscure the wordmark.
- **Motion** — the Circle can scale in/out, pulse, or rotate (full rotation only — never partial tilt). Wordmark always returns to upright, legible.

#### DO
- Use the Circle as the primary brand anchor on every asset.
- Use the master Magenta Circle wherever the brand needs to read as Sunglow first, range second.
- Use colour-coded variant Circles to identify product ranges (Section 3.4).
- Place the Circle with confidence — generous scale, generous space.

#### DO NOT
- Change the proportions of the Circle (no ovals, no rounded squares).
- Outline, drop-shadow (beyond the built-in dimensional edge), gradient overlay, or 3D treatments.
- Tilt or rotate the wordmark relative to the Circle.
- Stretch or skew either the Circle or the wordmark.
- Place the wordmark outside the Circle.
- Use the Circle in colours outside the approved palette (Section 3.4).
- Use the wordmark as a letter substitute in other words.
- Place over busy photography that compromises legibility of the wordmark.

### 3.2 The Sunglow Dot

When the Circle is too small to carry the wordmark legibly, the **standalone Sunglow Dot** (the Circle without the wordmark) is used as a brand-recognition cue. The Dot is used:
- As a favicon and app icon.
- As a brand-corner anchor on dense layouts.
- As a repeating pattern element.
- As a sign-off on social posts where the full Circle would crowd.

The Dot still follows all Circle construction rules — perfect proportions, approved colour, darker outline edge — and must always appear alongside the full Circle or wordmark somewhere on the asset (master pack, end card, profile image, etc.).

### 3.3 Tagline — "Let it glow."

A short, three-word brand sign-off.

- **Use with discipline.** Overuse drains its meaning. Showing the glow is more powerful than saying it.
- **Recommended use:** TVC end cards, hero campaign moments, master packaging back panels, signature brand films.
- **Set in:** Alatsi Regular, sentence case, always with the period.
- **Colour:** Magenta on White; White on Magenta, Deep Magenta, or any variant Circle colour; Charcoal on light photography.
- **Clearspace:** equal to the cap-height of the typeset tagline.
- **Minimum size:** Print 4 mm / Digital 14 px.
- **Localisation:** market-approved translation only. Confirm with local Legal before public use.

### 3.4 Colour Palette

Sunglow's colour system is **a fixed master palette plus a defined range palette.** The master palette signals "Sunglow"; the range palette signals "which Sunglow." Discipline lives in knowing which to use when.

#### Master Palette (Brand Identity)

| Colour | PMS (approx.) | CMYK | RGB | HEX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sunglow Magenta** | 220 C | 22.100.41.10 | 176.0.100 | **#B00064** |
| **Deep Magenta** | 2042 C | 27.100.46.21 | 149.1.85 | **#950155** |
| **Off-White** | — | 1.1.1.0 | 249.249.249 | **#F9F9F9** |

- **Sunglow Magenta** is the master brand colour — the colour the Circle wears when the brand needs to read as Sunglow before anything else. Used on corporate communications, master campaigns, and the brand's default identity.
- **Deep Magenta** is the depth partner — used for outlines on the Circle (the darker edge), hover states, type emphasis on light backgrounds, and second-tier surfaces.
- **Off-White** is the breathing-room base — the canvas, the page, the surface against which everything else lands.

#### Range Palette (Product Lines)

Each product range owns one variant colour. The Circle, packaging, and supporting design system shift to that colour while every other rule (wordmark in white, construction, typography) stays identical. This is how Sunglow flexes without losing itself.

| Range | HEX | Tone | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sunshine Yellow** | **#FFC72C** | Light & moisture | Daily moisturising shampoos, conditioners, leave-ins |
| **Glow Orange** | **#F26E36** | Strength & repair | Strengthening, breakage-protection range |
| **Radiance Red** | **#E03C2D** | Intensive treatment | Deep-conditioning masks, scalp treatments |
| **Forest Green** | **#2D6F49** | Natural & herbal | Plant-based, sulphate-free, scalp-care range |
| **Royal Purple** | **#5E2C8C** | Premium & toning | Toning shampoos, premium treatments, gift sets |

Range colours appear:
- On the Circle for that range's products.
- On the packaging primary surface.
- As campaign accent for range-led work.
- On in-store wayfinding and shelf strips for that range.

Range colours **never** appear:
- On the master Sunglow Circle (which is always Magenta).
- As the dominant colour of a master-brand asset (Sunglow Magenta leads there).
- In combination with other range colours in the same asset (one range per composition).
- As an off-brand variant invented for a single campaign without Global Brand approval.

#### Functional Palette (Limited Use)

| Colour | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Charcoal** | #1A1A1A | Body type on Off-White surfaces |
| **Soft Gray** | #E5E5E5 | UI dividers, secondary surfaces, recipe-card backgrounds |
| **Black** | #000000 | Legal type and fine print only |

The functional palette is the **only** acceptable extension of the master and range palettes. No additional brand colours, no decorative tints, no gradient variants.

#### Colour Proportion Rules
- **Sunglow Magenta dominant** in master-brand work; **the range colour dominant** in range-led work.
- **Off-White as primary background** for long-form digital, packaging text panels, and clean editorial.
- **Deep Magenta sparingly** — Circle outlines, type emphasis, second-tier surfaces. Never as a primary background.
- Generous breathing room. Negative space is brand-active.

#### Engagement-Based Colour Volume

| Engagement Length | Description | Brand Volume | Product Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Glance** (≤3s) | OOH, flash digital, end cards | 10 | 1 |
| **Browse** (1–2 min) | Social, banner, shelf-on-shelf | 6 | 5 |
| **Routine** (2+ min) | Recipe page, in-app, packaging back | 2 | 10 |

#### Colour DO / DO NOT
- **DO** anchor every master-brand asset with the Sunglow Magenta Circle.
- **DO** let a range colour lead when the campaign is range-led — the Circle adopts that colour for the moment.
- **DO** use Off-White as the primary breathing-room base.
- **DO** maintain darker-outline-edge on every Circle, in every colour.
- **DO NOT** introduce colours outside the master or range palettes.
- **DO NOT** tint, gradient, or fade brand colours.
- **DO NOT** combine two range colours in the same composition.
- **DO NOT** use range colours on the master Sunglow Circle (corporate, master campaigns).
- **DO NOT** use Sunshine Yellow or Glow Orange as type on Off-White (insufficient contrast — both fail WCAG AA).

#### Accessibility
- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum for all body copy: 4.5:1 contrast.
- **White on Sunglow Magenta** — passes AAA. Primary type pairing for Magenta surfaces.
- **White on Deep Magenta** — passes AAA.
- **Charcoal on Off-White** — passes AAA. Primary body-text pairing.
- **White on Forest Green / Royal Purple** — passes AA at all sizes.
- **White on Radiance Red** — passes AA for body and AAA for large text.
- **White on Sunshine Yellow** — **fails contrast.** Use Charcoal on Yellow instead.
- **White on Glow Orange** — **borderline.** Use Charcoal for body copy; White acceptable for large display only.
- Never rely on Circle colour alone to identify a product range — always pair with the range name in type.

### 3.5 Typography

Sunglow uses **one typeface: Alatsi.** Single weight, single voice. The discipline of one font is the brand's typographic signature — hierarchy comes from size, case, colour, tracking, and space, not from weight stacks.

#### The Single-Weight System
Alatsi is a humanist sans with friendly geometry, designed for clarity at any size. Without multiple weights, the brand's hierarchy works through:
- **Size** — the primary differentiator. Headlines are large; body is small. The gap is generous.
- **Case** — UPPERCASE for category labels and section markers; sentence case for everything readable.
- **Colour** — Magenta for emphasis on Off-White; White on Magenta or any range colour.
- **Tracking** — slightly loose for display; standard for body; tight only for UI labels.
- **Space** — the room around the type does the work bold would do elsewhere.

#### Type Roles

| Element | Style |
|---|---|
| Hero headline (TVC, OOH, hero social) | Alatsi at large display size, sentence case, tight leading (1.0–1.1×) |
| Section header | Alatsi UPPERCASE, slightly tracked (+50 to +100), small to mid scale |
| Subhead | Alatsi at mid scale, sentence case, leading 1.2× |
| Body | Alatsi at 16 px+ digital / 9 pt+ print, leading 1.5× |
| Emphasis within body | Sunglow Magenta colour (not italics, not bold) |
| Quote / sensory descriptor | Alatsi italic (where licensed) or Alatsi at larger size, Magenta |
| Legal / back-of-pack | Alatsi at 7–8 pt, leading 1.3× |
| UI label / button | Alatsi UPPERCASE, tracked +25, single line |

#### Typesetting Rules
- **Headlines:** max 8 words. Sentence case. Tight leading. Generous space around.
- **Subheads:** sentence case, 1.2× leading.
- **Body:** sentence case, 1.5× leading for routine-context copy (how-tos, ingredient guides, hair advice). Readability while in the shower or at the mirror matters.
- **Emphasis:** use Sunglow Magenta colour, not weight or italics. The reader's eye finds emphasis through colour, not boldness.
- **Numerals:** tabular figures in ingredient lists, price flags, and step-by-step routines. Lining figures elsewhere.
- **Tracking discipline:** never default-tracked at large display sizes — Alatsi at 60 pt+ needs −10 to −25 to look right. Body sits at default tracking. UI labels and section headers track +25 to +100.

#### Print Type/Colour Pairings (DO)

| Surface | Type colour |
|---|---|
| Off-White | Charcoal, Sunglow Magenta, or Deep Magenta |
| Sunglow Magenta | White or Off-White |
| Deep Magenta | White or Off-White |
| Range colours (any) | White, except Sunshine Yellow (Charcoal) and Glow Orange (Charcoal for body) |
| Light photography | Charcoal or Sunglow Magenta |
| Dark photography | White or Off-White |

#### Digital Type/Colour Pairings (DO)

| Surface | Type colour |
|---|---|
| Off-White | Charcoal (body), Sunglow Magenta (emphasis & headlines) |
| Sunglow Magenta | White |
| Range colours | White (Yellow/Orange use Charcoal for body) |
| Photography (light) | Charcoal |
| Photography (dark) | White |

#### Forbidden in digital body copy
- Sunglow Magenta type at sizes under 14 px (legibility).
- Sunshine Yellow or Glow Orange type on Off-White (insufficient contrast).
- White type on Sunshine Yellow (fails contrast).
- ALL-CAPS body copy (reserved for section labels and pack legal).
- Fake bolding via text-shadow or font-weight on browsers without a true Alatsi Bold.

#### Licensing
Alatsi is an Open Font License typeface (Google Fonts) — free for commercial use across all Sunglow applications including digital, print, broadcast, app, and packaging. No additional licence required.

If a display or partnership font is ever proposed (limited-edition campaign, chef-style collaboration), Legal approval is required and the licence must explicitly cover the intended application.

### 3.6 Imagery & Photography

This is where Sunglow lives or dies. The category has a long history of getting Black hair wrong — bad lighting, flattening textures, single-tone retouching, "tame and control" framing. We do the opposite.

#### Hair Photography Principles

- **Hair is the subject.** Hair fills the frame. Faces appear, but hair is what we're here for.
- **Light for the hair.** Lighting setups should reveal coil definition, strand separation, and natural shine. Soft, directional, generous. Never flat overhead light that erases texture.
- **Show the texture honestly.** Frizz, halo, baby hairs, edge work, parting, scalp — all welcome. We don't airbrush texture into a single smooth surface.
- **Real shine, not retouched shine.** If the product delivers glow, the camera should capture it without a post-production gloss layer. Substantiated claims live or die on this.
- **Show the range.** A complete brand photo set covers, at minimum: 4a, 4b, 4c, loose curls, locs, braids, protective styles, relaxed hair, transitioning hair, and a range of lengths.
- **Show the moments.** Wash day, leave-in application, the bonnet, the silk press, the twist-out reveal, the edges, the puff, the protective style.

#### Lifestyle Photography Principles

- **Joy first.** Sunglow's lifestyle imagery defaults to ease, confidence, and warmth. Even the practical moments (wash day) are celebrated, not endured.
- **Inclusive casting within Black communities.** Diasporic range — African, Afro-Caribbean, African-American, Afro-Latina, mixed-heritage — across ages, body types, and gender expression.
- **Real spaces.** A real bathroom, a real bedroom, a real braider's chair. Avoid sterile studio backdrops for lifestyle.
- **Hands in frame.** Show care happening — fingers detangling, a partner braiding, mum doing daughter's hair, the friend who lays edges.
- **Mirror moments.** The look-in-the-mirror beat is a brand signature. Confidence meeting hair, not surprise meeting transformation.

#### Composition & Emotion Rules

- Generous space around the subject. Let hair breathe in the frame.
- Warm, intentional light — golden, soft, never harsh.
- Earned spontaneity (a smile mid-laugh, hands mid-style) over staged poses.
- Avoid: cold beauty-counter studio aesthetics, "before/after" trauma narratives, white-gaze framing of Black hair as exotic, motion blur that hides texture, monochrome edits that strip colour from skin.

#### Hair Representation — Non-Negotiables

- **Never** style or describe Black hair as needing to be "tamed," "controlled," "fixed," or "managed."
- **Never** use straightening or smoothing as the "after" image in a transformation narrative.
- **Never** use a white-hair "before" / Black-hair "after" structure or its reverse.
- **Always** credit the hairstylist by name in editorial and social.
- **Always** caption with the specific texture, style, and (where appropriate) product used.
- **Always** ensure model talent is paid at category-appropriate rates and treated by hair professionals who specialise in Black hair.

### 3.7 Iconography & Graphics

- **Soft, rounded geometry** — same DNA as the Circle.
- 2 px stroke at 24 px size; scale proportionally.
- Outline icons for UI; filled icons for hair-type indicators and product-range markers.
- Permitted illustrative motifs: hair-strand lines, soft sun rays (referencing "sun" + "glow"), coil curls, droplets, bonnet silhouettes.
- Always supporting the photography and the Circle — never competing.

### 3.8 Sonic Identity *(optional, in development)*

If sonic is rolled out, the brand mnemonic should evoke warmth and lift — a soft chime or a rising two-note motif. Audio sign-off after the tagline. Pure form maintained in Always-on; reinterpretations allowed in Brand Moments with approval.

## 4. Product Presentation Guidelines

### 4.1 Styling Principles
- **Hero the result first.** The hair, the glow, the texture. Pack appears as supporting witness.
- **When pack leads** (e.g., NPD, value), show product cleanly against the range colour or Off-White — never against busy photography.
- **Show the application moment** — a palm-full of conditioner, the bottle squeezed, the leave-in pumped into a curl. Action reads stronger than still.

### 4.2 Realism vs Aspiration
Sunglow sits at **honest aspiration**. The result on screen must be achievable by a real wearer of the product. We do not retouch coil patterns smoother, paint in shine that the formula can't deliver, or imply outcomes the product hasn't been substantiated against.

### 4.3 Consistency Across Markets
- The Circle, the wordmark, and the master palette appear identically on every pack worldwide.
- Pack architecture (Circle placement, range colour band, hero photography) follows a single global template.
- Local diasporic communities are honoured in casting, hair type representation, and ingredient cues — a Caribbean market's photography reflects Caribbean Black hair stories, a Nigerian market's reflects West African hair stories.
- Variant typography, layout, and brand voice remain globally consistent.

## 5. Messaging & Copywriting Framework

### 5.1 Messaging Pillars

Every campaign ladders up to one or more:

1. **Made For** — formulated for Black hair from the start.
2. **Real Glow** — visible, substantiated shine and health.
3. **Every Texture** — the breadth of Black hair celebrated, named, and served.
4. **Care, Not Rescue** — Black hair is loved, not fixed.

### 5.2 Language Patterns
- **Short sentences.** Headlines under 8 words wherever possible.
- **Specific over generic.** "For 4c coils" beats "for textured hair." "Strengthens edges" beats "supports healthy hair."
- **Active, present tense.** "Lays the edges. Holds the day." not "It will help you achieve laid edges."
- **Hair-care vocabulary used correctly.** Slip, definition, hold, frizz halo, porosity, breakage, retention, density, scalp tension, protective style, twist-out — used precisely, not for flavour.
- **Numbers when honest.** Substantiated only: "78% reported less breakage in 4 weeks" — always linked to the study.

### 5.3 Emotional Triggers
- **Confidence** (the mirror moment)
- **Belonging** (you are the centre, not the special case)
- **Ease** (wash day works)
- **Joy** (hair is fun, not a project)
- **Pride** (the style, the texture, the heritage)

### 5.4 Tagline Discipline
- "Let it glow." is a closing line — TVC end cards, signature brand campaigns, master packaging.
- Not used as a closer on every social post or banner.
- Always set in Alatsi Regular with the period.
- Localised translations only when Legal-approved.

### 5.5 Localisation Guidelines
- **Translate the truth, not the words.** A "wash day" insight in one market may become a "co-wash day" insight in another, or a "salon Saturday" insight elsewhere.
- **Lead with local Black hair stories.** Hero imagery on local packs and campaigns reflects the textures, styling traditions, and cultural cues of the market.
- **Local talent, local stylists.** Casting and hair work must reflect the market it appears in, paid at appropriate rates.
- **Pricing and pack sizes localise; brand architecture does not.**

## 6. Audience Segmentation

Sunglow's audience is **Black women, men, and non-binary people across the diaspora.** The segmentation below cuts by relationship with hair, not by demographics — because the same person moves through multiple segments over a lifetime.

### 6.1 The Routine Builder
- **Insight:** Has a regimen. Knows what works, what doesn't, where the gaps are. Reads ingredient lists.
- **Hooks:** Specific-claim products, regimen-building content, full-range awareness.
- **Tone:** Specific, peer-to-peer, ingredient-literate. "The hold you've been measuring for."

### 6.2 The Transitioner
- **Insight:** Moving from relaxed to natural, or rebuilding after damage. Needs reassurance and structure.
- **Hooks:** Protein/moisture balance, breakage protection, transitioning-friendly content.
- **Tone:** Warm, patient, expert-without-pressure. "Your hair is figuring itself out. Here's what helps."

### 6.3 The Protective-Style Wearer
- **Insight:** Lives in braids, locs, weaves, wigs for stretches. Scalp health and edges matter.
- **Hooks:** Scalp care, edge serums, leave-ins compatible with installs, between-style recovery.
- **Tone:** Practical, scalp-aware, real-talk. "Your scalp is still doing work under there."

### 6.4 The New Natural
- **Insight:** Recent big chop, new to texture, asking everything for the first time.
- **Hooks:** Starter sets, education-led content, friendly product matching.
- **Tone:** Generous, never patronising, welcome-in. "Start here. Your hair will tell you the rest."

### 6.5 The Heritage Wearer
- **Insight:** Hair is family history, identity, ritual. Often multi-generational in shared products and routines.
- **Hooks:** Premium ranges, gift sets, multi-generational content, heritage-rooted storytelling.
- **Tone:** Honouring, intentional, story-led. "What grandma used. Made for now."

## 7. Campaign System Framework

### 7.1 Campaign Portfolio Framework

| Tier | Share | Role | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Always-on / Core** | 65% | Sustaining presence across the range. Routine content, in-store, value, education. | Wash-day series, ingredient explainers, range awareness, app deals. |
| **Brand Moments** | 25% | Tentpole campaigns that celebrate Black hair culture or own a category moment. | Crown Day, Wash Day Season, Hair Heritage Month, Mother-Daughter campaign. |
| **Partnerships / Collabs** | 10% | Co-created with stylists, creators, or cultural collaborators. | Stylist-collab limited editions, creator-led routines, salon partnerships. |

### 7.2 Campaign Types

#### 7.2.1 Product Launch
- Hero the result, then the new product.
- One Hair Truth as hero message.
- The product's range colour earns its visual moment.
- TVC + social + tutorial content + in-store + pack reveal — synchronised.

#### 7.2.2 Seasonal / Cultural Moment
- Tied to a real cultural moment (Crown Day, Hair Heritage Month, back-to-school braids, festival season).
- Always begins with the lived moment, not the product.
- Often a Brand Moment with treated Circle latitude (with approval).

#### 7.2.3 Value Campaign
- Bold Alatsi typography on Off-White or Sunglow Magenta.
- Clear price, clear pack, clear benefit.
- The range colour signals which range. Sunglow Magenta or White anchors the brand.
- Copy: ≤6 words headline, single CTA.

#### 7.2.4 Brand Campaign
- Long-form storytelling. Permission for warmth, joy, and pride.
- Hero is always the wearer and the hair. Pack appears only as a witness.
- Tagline used at sign-off — earned, not pasted.

#### 7.2.5 Stylist / Creator Partnership
- Co-created within Sunglow's rules. The Circle stays Magenta unless the partnership is range-led (then it adopts that range's colour).
- Partner's voice and Sunglow's voice intersect; Sunglow is the host, not the louder voice.
- Always result-led: the partnership's reason for existing is the hair.

### 7.3 Campaign Components

Every campaign has:

1. **Hero Message** — one Hair Truth or product truth, max 8 words.
2. **Supporting Message** — 1–2 lines proving or extending the hero, no more.
3. **Visual System** — hair-led hero photography + Sunglow Circle (master or range) + range colour where applicable.
4. **CTA Pattern** — verb-led, ≤3 words: "Find Your Range" / "Shop the Routine" / "See the Style" / "Save the Tutorial."
5. **Sign-off** — Circle + (optional) tagline.

### 7.5 Content Templates

#### 7.5.1 Hair-Led Instagram Post
- **Visual:** Close to the hair. Texture in focus. Sunglow Circle bottom-right or top-left.
- **Caption:** Headline (Hair Truth, ≤8 words) + result (1 line) + product credit + CTA ("Save the Routine").
- **Format:** Carousel preferred — slide 1 result, slide 2 product used, slide 3 step-by-step, slide 4 wearer credit + tag.

#### 7.5.2 Video Ad Storyboard (20s)
- **0–3s:** Hair close-up — shine, coil definition, movement. Sound: ambient, optional voiceover open.
- **3–10s:** A wearer's moment (mirror, hands in hair, finished style). One Hair Truth voiced or supered.
- **10–15s:** The Sunglow moment — product in palm, in hair, working.
- **15–18s:** The finished glow — mirror beat, the wearer, light.
- **18–20s:** Circle + tagline ("Let it glow.") + sonic sign-off.

#### 7.5.3 Banner Ad (Static)
- **Layout:** Hair hero left, Circle top-right, headline + CTA right.
- **Colour:** Off-White base, range colour accent for range-led campaigns, Sunglow Magenta for master brand.
- **Headline:** ≤6 words. CTA: ≤3 words. No tagline.

#### 7.5.4 OOH / Billboard
- **Asset:** Hair-led hero filling the canvas, or the Circle at scale on Sunglow Magenta.
- **Headline:** ≤5 words. Hair Truth or sensory line.
- **No CTA.** Brand recall is the CTA.

#### 7.5.5 In-app Push Notification
- Subject ≤30 chars ("Wash day, sorted."). Body ≤90 chars. One CTA verb. No tagline.

#### 7.5.6 Email
- Subject ≤45 chars. Preheader ≤90 chars. Hair-led hero. Single primary CTA ("Shop the Routine"). Circle in footer.

#### 7.5.7 Packaging (Master Architecture)
- **Front:** Sunglow Circle (in range colour) top-centre. Range name in Alatsi UPPERCASE below Circle. Product name in Alatsi sentence case. Hero hair shot or texture detail filling the lower half.
- **Back:** Hair Truth or product promise line at top, full ingredient list (INCI) in Alatsi at 7–8 pt, application steps in numbered list, allergens and legal warnings in Alatsi Regular at 7 pt.
- **Side:** "Let it glow." tagline + minimal volume / pack-size strip.

## 8. Digital & UX Guidelines

### 8.1 App & Website Tone
- **Routine-led utility first**, warmth second. Get the wearer to the right product quickly, then add the friend-who-knows-your-hair touch.
- Error states stay human: "Couldn't find that routine — let's try a different texture?" — not "Search returned no results."
- Confirmations specific: "Your moisture routine is saved. We'll remind you Sunday."

### 8.2 UI Consistency Principles
- Off-White as primary surface; Sunglow Magenta for primary actions, wayfinding, brand moments.
- Range colours for range-specific contexts (a product detail page in the Sunshine Yellow range adopts Yellow accents; a Forest Green range page adopts Green).
- Charcoal type on Off-White for body throughout digital.
- Circle used as a UI module for routine cards, tip callouts, and saved-item containers.
- Alatsi throughout. Single weight, with hierarchy through size and case.

### 8.3 Microcopy Guidelines
- **CTAs:** verb-led, ≤3 words. "Build Routine," "Match My Hair," "Find In-Store," "Save for Later."
- **Empty states:** warm, specific. "No saved routines yet — let's match your hair to a starter set →"
- **Confirmations:** human and specific. "Sunshine Yellow leave-in added to your cart."
- **Loading:** brief. A brand wink permitted only at long waits (>2s): "Pouring the glow…"

### 8.4 Accessibility
- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum.
- Alt text on every hair image — descriptive ("a close-up of defined 4b coils with visible shine") not promotional. Caption texture and style honestly.
- Routine and ingredient lists usable by screen readers, with structured numbered lists and clear heading hierarchy.
- Don't rely on Circle colour alone to identify range — always pair with range name in type.
- Subtitles on all video tutorial content; audio description where relevant.

## 10. Compliance & Brand Safety

### 10.1 Legal Approvals
- **All advertising materials** require clearance by local Sunglow counsel or Global Marketing/IP Legal before publication.
- **Treated Circle or wordmark** uses (off-palette colours, structural variations, partnership co-locks) require Global Brand + Legal IP approval at concept and pre-final stages.
- **New trademarks, slogans, or range names** require Legal review before any external use.

### 10.2 Fonts & Licensing
- Alatsi (Open Font License via Google Fonts) is the licensed Sunglow typeface, free for commercial use across all applications.
- Any non-Alatsi usage (display partnership fonts, limited editions) requires a verified commercial-use licence covering the application and Legal sign-off.

### 10.3 Cosmetic Claims & Imagery
- **All performance claims** ("reduces breakage," "78% more shine," "for 24-hour moisture") require substantiation by clinical study or consumer-perception research per market regulation. Always verify with Regulatory before publication.
- **Imagery must reflect achievable results.** No retouched shine that the formula cannot deliver. No retouched coil patterns that the product does not produce. Before/after imagery must be from the same lighting and post-treatment timing, with talent release and substantiation on file.
- **INCI ingredient lists** are mandatory on pack and digital product pages per local regulation.
- **Allergen and fragrance disclosure** follow local regulation (EU FIC, US FDA, etc.).
- **Sustainability claims** require documented supply-chain and lifecycle verification. Vague claims ("clean," "natural," "eco-friendly") without specifics are off-brand and high-risk legally.

### 10.4 Cultural & Representational Safety
- Casting, hair work, and storytelling must reflect the Black communities the brand serves — across diasporic identity, age, body type, and gender expression.
- Stylists working on Sunglow shoots must be experienced with the textures and styles being shot. No "best-effort" stylists working on hair they're not trained for.
- Avoid: rescue narratives, before/after trauma framing, exoticisation, white-gaze framing of Black hair, single-texture representation across a campaign.
- Hair representation issues escalate to Global Brand immediately — these are brand-existential, not tonal.

### 10.5 Confidentiality
Internal use only across the Sunglow System: employees, franchisees, agencies, suppliers, distributors, salon partners. Not for external distribution without Legal approval.

### 11.2 Campaign Generation Rules

Any generated campaign **must**:

1. **Map to one portfolio tier** — Always-on / Brand Moment / Partnership (65/25/10).
2. **Hero the hair, not the pack.** Pack is a witness, not the headline.
3. **Anchor on one Hair Truth** — lived, shared, resolved by Sunglow.
4. **Apply the right Circle colour:**
   - Master Magenta for corporate, master campaigns, brand moments.
   - Range colour when the campaign is range-led.
5. **Use colour proportion** appropriate to engagement length:
   - Glance (≤3s): brand 10, product 1
   - Browse (1–2 min): brand 6, product 5
   - Routine (2+ min): brand 2, product 10
6. **Use Alatsi only.** Hierarchy through size, case, colour, tracking — never via bold or shadow tricks.
7. **One range colour per composition.** Never mix two range colours.
8. **Tagline reserved for sign-off.** Not used in workhorse comms.
9. **CTA verb-led, ≤3 words.**
10. **Pass the friend-who-knows-your-hair voice test.**
11. **No forbidden language** — never "tame," "control," "fix," "transformation," "magic," "miracle."
12. **No regulated performance claims** without Regulatory clearance.
13. **Hair representation non-negotiables apply to every image** (Section 3.6) — flag any generated brief that risks violating them.
