# Haleon Brand Guidelines

**Enterprise Masterbrand & Portfolio System**
*Version 1.0 — Built for corporate marketing, brand teams across the portfolio, agencies, and AI-driven campaign generation systems.*

> **Brand context.** Haleon is a world-leading, pure-play consumer health company, 100% focused on everyday health. It combines trusted science, innovation, and deep human understanding to make health and wellness products used by millions worldwide — through a portfolio of nine Power Brands and many Local Growth Brands across six categories, led publicly by **Sensodyne, Advil, and Centrum**. > **Document purpose.** A single source of truth for the Haleon corporate masterbrand — its identity, voice, and the rules for how it appears alongside its product brands. It does not replace the individual brand guidelines of Sensodyne, Advil, Centrum, and the rest of the portfolio.

## 0. Masterbrand Architecture (READ FIRST)

Haleon is a **masterbrand parent to a house of brands.** Three structural rules govern everything below:

1. **Haleon is the company; the product brands are the heroes at shelf.** In consumer advertising, the product brand (Sensodyne, Advil, Centrum, etc.) usually leads. Haleon appears as the trusted parent — an endorsement, a corporate signature, a source of credibility — not usually as the headline in product advertising.
2. **Product-brand guidelines govern product-brand work.** Where this Haleon masterbrand guideline meets an individual brand's own guidelines on that brand's own advertising, the product-brand guideline leads for brand-specific expression. This document governs the Haleon masterbrand and the endorsement relationship.
3. **Health-claim and regulatory compliance is absolute.** Consumer health is one of the most heavily regulated advertising categories in the world. Every asset, in every market, must comply with that market's health-product advertising regulation and with the rules in Section 10. This overrides creative ambition and deadlines, without exception.

Where any of the three is uncertain for a given piece of work, it does not proceed until resolved with brand and regulatory/legal.

## 2. Brand Personality & Voice

### 2.1 Brand Archetype
**Caregiver** with a **Sage** foundation. Haleon cares (it exists to help people be well) and it knows (it's grounded in science). The blend is the brand: warmth backed by evidence, expertise delivered with humanity.

### 2.2 Personality Attributes
- **Caring** — genuinely on the side of people's health.
- **Credible** — science-grounded, evidence-led.
- **Empowering** — puts control in people's hands.
- **Clear** — accessible, never clinical or cold.
- **Inclusive** — for everyone, barriers removed.

### 2.3 Tone of Voice
Consistent across corporate, employer, investor, and endorsement contexts:
- **Caring and empathetic** — people first, always.
- **Evidence-based and trustworthy** — claims we can stand behind.
- **Positive and empowering** — health as possibility, not fear.
- **Clear and accessible** — plain language, no jargon, no gatekeeping.

### 2.4 Voice Principle
**Science you can trust, said the way a person would say it.**

Not a lab report, not a pharmacy leaflet, not a fear-based ad. A knowledgeable, warm human translating real science into everyday help. Every line should hold both credibility and humanity at once.

### 2.5 Creative Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Science with humanity** | Every message earns its credibility from evidence and its warmth from human understanding. Never one without the other — cold science or empty warmth are both off-brand. |
| **Everyday, not exceptional** | We're about the small, daily acts of health — the toothache handled, the vitamins remembered, the good night's sleep. Not dramatic medical rescue. The power is in the ordinary. |
| **Health made inclusive** | We design to remove barriers — in language, in accessibility, in representation, in access. Better health for everyone is the test, not better health for some. |

### 2.6 Health Truths

We organise masterbrand creative around small, real, human moments of everyday health. A great Health Truth is:
- **Everyday** — a real, ordinary health moment, not a dramatic one.
- **Human** — felt by real people, told with empathy.
- **Empowering** — points to someone taking control, not being rescued.

Examples:
- "The difference a good night's sleep makes to everything else."
- "Taking care of the small things before they become big ones."
- "Health advice that doesn't need a medical degree to understand."
- "The confidence of a smile that doesn't hold back."

### 2.7 Do's and Don'ts of Communication

**DO**
- Pair evidence with empathy in every message.
- Use plain, accessible, inclusive language.
- Empower people to take control of their own health.
- Be specific and honest about what a product does — and only what it does.
- Represent the full diversity of the people we serve.

**DON'T**
- Overpromise, imply cures, or overstate benefits (see Section 10 — every health claim must be substantiated and compliant).
- Use fear, shame, or anxiety to sell.
- Sound clinical, cold, or gatekept.
- Diagnose, or position products as substitutes for professional medical advice where that would be inappropriate.
- Exclude, stereotype, or make health feel like it's for some people and not others.

### 2.8 Context Flexing — Where the Haleon Masterbrand Speaks

The Haleon masterbrand voice appears in specific contexts, distinct from product-brand advertising:

| Context | Role | Register |
|---|---|---|
| **Corporate & purpose** | The company, its purpose, its impact | Warm, credible, purpose-led |
| **Employer brand** | Attracting talent | Human, ambitious, inclusive |
| **Investor & ESG** | Performance, sustainability, governance | Confident, precise, evidence-led |
| **Endorsement (on product)** | The trusted parent behind a product brand | Understated, credibility-lending, never overshadowing the product brand |
| **Health inclusivity / social** | Removing barriers to health | Empathetic, empowering, action-led |

### 2.9 Example Messaging
- "Better everyday health for everyone."
- "Science with humanity."
- "Health made more inclusive."
- "Everyday health, in more hands."
- **Signature line:** "Better everyday health with humanity." — the purpose line, used as the masterbrand sign-off.

## 3. Visual Identity System

### 3.1 Logo — The Haleon Wordmark

The Haleon identity is the **Haleon wordmark** — "HALEON" in bold, credible letterforms — with its signature **green "E"**: the middle horizontal bar of the letter E rendered in bright Haleon green. This green stroke is the brand's most ownable visual asset — a single note of optimism and energy inside an otherwise black (or white) wordmark.

#### Official Master Artwork
The approved vector logo files are held in the **`Logos/`** folder (see the asset map in §0.1):

| File | Colourway | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `Logos/haleon_logo_black.svg` | Black wordmark + green "E" bar (**#30EA03**) | Light / white grounds |
| `Logos/haleon_logo_white.svg` | White wordmark + green "E" bar (**#30EA03**) | Dark / image / green grounds |

Both are the full HALEON wordmark at a 1426 × 222 viewBox, scalable losslessly. **Always place the supplied vector** — never recreate, re-space, or re-weight the wordmark, and never rebuild the green "E" by hand.

#### Anatomy
- **The Wordmark** — "HALEON" set in the brand's bold, even-weight letterforms.
- **The Green "E"** — the middle horizontal bar of the "E" is set in Haleon logo green **#30EA03** (a vivid signal green). This is the signature device — the spark inside the wordmark. The rest of the wordmark is black (on light) or white (on dark).

> **Note on greens.** The *logo* green is **#30EA03** (the vivid green baked into the master artwork). The broader brand/communications green used for the highlight-bar device, headline highlights, and UI accents is **Haleon Green #65AC1E** (see §3.4). Keep the logo's green as supplied in the artwork; use #65AC1E for the graphic-highlight system around it. Do not recolour the logo's "E" to #65AC1E or vice-versa.

#### Construction Rules (non-negotiable)
1. **The green "E" bar is always present and always #30EA03** in the logo. It is not decorative — it is core to the mark.
2. **The wordmark and green-"E" proportions are fixed.** Use approved master artwork; never recreate, re-space, or re-weight.
3. **Two core colourways:** black wordmark + green "E" on light grounds (`haleon_logo_black.svg`); white wordmark + green "E" on dark grounds (`haleon_logo_white.svg`).

#### Naming
Refer to the mark as **"the Haleon wordmark"** and the device as **"the green highlight."** Together they are **"the Haleon logo."**

#### Clear Space
- Clearspace equals **the cap-height of the wordmark**, applied on all four sides.
- Minimum size: **Print 15 mm** / **Digital 90 px** wide (wordmark).

#### Trademark
Carry ® / ™ per Haleon legal standards on external commercial materials. Not required on internal-only materials.

#### Endorsement Lockups
When Haleon endorses a product brand (e.g., on-pack, in a corporate frame), the endorsement follows the approved **"a Haleon company/brand"** style lockup:
- The product brand leads at its own size and identity.
- The Haleon endorsement appears smaller, in a fixed relationship, in an approved position (typically a corner or base).
- Never scale the Haleon endorsement to compete with the product brand.
- Never apply Haleon's green highlight to a product brand's logo.
- Endorsement lockups require Haleon Global Brand + the product-brand team's approval.

#### DO
- Use the wordmark with its green highlight as the corporate brand anchor.
- Use the correct colourway for the ground.
- Give the logo generous clear space.
- Use approved endorsement lockups when signing product-brand work.

#### DO NOT
- Remove, recolour, or restyle the green highlight.
- Recreate or re-space the wordmark.
- Apply the highlight device to product-brand logos.
- Scale the Haleon endorsement to overshadow a product brand.
- Add effects (shadow, gradient, bevel, glow).
- Stretch, skew, or rotate any part.
- Place on busy backgrounds that compromise legibility.

### 3.2 The Green Highlight as a Graphic Device

The green highlight extends into a **graphic system** for masterbrand contexts:
- **The Highlight** — the green block used to emphasise a key word in a headline (like a marker highlight), signalling optimism and importance.
- **The Underline** — a green rule under a word or section, echoing the logo device.
- **Motion** — the highlight can "draw on" (a marker-stroke reveal) as a signature masterbrand animation.

When used as a graphic device:
- Always Haleon Green (#65AC1E).
- Used sparingly — one highlighted idea per composition. Highlighting everything highlights nothing.
- Reserved for the Haleon masterbrand; not applied within product-brand advertising unless the product brand's own guidelines adopt it.

### 3.3 Signature Line — "Better everyday health with humanity."

Haleon's purpose line, used as the masterbrand sign-off.

- **Use with purpose.** A sign-off for corporate, purpose, employer, and endorsement moments — not a tagline stamped on every asset.
- **Set in:** New Hero Medium or Bold, sentence case.
- **Colour:** Charcoal or Black on light grounds; White on dark grounds; the word "health" or "humanity" may carry the Haleon Green highlight where emphasis is wanted (sparingly).
- **Clearspace:** equal to the cap-height of the line.
- **Localisation:** market-approved translation; confirm with local brand and legal.

### 3.4 Colour Palette

Haleon's palette is **a clean, credible core plus an expressive secondary set** — green as the signature, black/white/charcoal for clarity and credibility, and a vivid secondary palette for energy, categories, and life.

#### Primary Palette (Core)

| Colour | Role | RGB | HEX |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Haleon Green** | The brand signature — the highlight, key accents | 101.172.30 | **#65AC1E** |
| **Black** | Primary type, structure, credibility | 0.0.0 | **#000000** |
| **White** | Primary background, space, clarity | 255.255.255 | **#FFFFFF** |
| **Charcoal** | Type, dark surfaces, warmer-than-black structure | 51.62.72 | **#333E48** |

- **Haleon Green** is the brand's signature spark — optimism, growth, health, energy. Used as the highlight device and key accent, not as a flooded background.
- **Black & White** are the credibility core — clean, clear, accessible. White-dominant layouts with black type read as trustworthy and modern.
- **Charcoal (#333E48)** is a warmer structural dark — a softer alternative to pure black for large surfaces and body type, keeping the brand human rather than stark.

#### Secondary Palette (Expressive)

| Colour | RGB | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Magenta** | 250.1.254 | **#FA01FE** | Energy, category coding, data highlights, expressive accents |
| **Lime Yellow** | 219.254.2 | **#DBFE02** | Vitality, category coding, highlights |
| **Purple** | 172.123.255 | **#AC7BFF** | Warmth, category coding, expressive accents |
| **Teal** | 92.224.202 | **#5CE0CA** | Calm, category coding, expressive accents |

The secondary palette adds energy, humanity, and a system for coding categories, campaigns, or content types. Used as **accents and coding**, never replacing Haleon Green as the brand signature and never overwhelming the clean core.

#### Neutral / Functional Palette

| Colour | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Gray 700** | #6E7579 | Secondary type, captions |
| **Gray 300** | #CFD2D3 | Dividers, borders, disabled states |
| **Gray 100** | #F2F3F3 | Soft backgrounds, cards |

#### Colour Proportion Rules
- **White-dominant** layouts — clean, credible, accessible. Space is a trust signal.
- **Haleon Green as the signature accent** — the highlight, key CTAs, brand moments. Green leads the accent role but doesn't flood.
- **Black / Charcoal for type and structure.**
- **Secondary palette as expressive accents or category coding** — one or two per composition, never a rainbow.
- Generous breathing room throughout.

#### Colour DO / DO NOT
- **DO** build clean, white-dominant, accessible layouts.
- **DO** use the Haleon Green highlight as the signature spark.
- **DO** use the secondary palette to code categories or add energy — sparingly.
- **DO** use Charcoal as a warmer structural dark.
- **DO NOT** flood layouts with Haleon Green or any secondary colour.
- **DO NOT** combine many secondary colours in one composition (max two).
- **DO NOT** use the vivid secondary colours for body text (most fail contrast — see accessibility).
- **DO NOT** apply Haleon Green or the highlight device to product-brand logos.

#### Accessibility (health = access for all)
Accessibility is a brand value for a company whose purpose is inclusive health. WCAG 2.1 AA is the floor; AAA for core health-information flows.
- **Black on White** — AAA. Primary body pairing.
- **Charcoal (#333E48) on White** — AAA. Warm body pairing.
- **White on Charcoal / Black** — AAA.
- **Haleon Green (#65AC1E) on White** — passes AA for large text (18 px+/24 px bold); borderline for body — prefer Black/Charcoal for body, use Green for headlines and accents.
- **White on Haleon Green** — passes AA for large text; verify for body; prefer Charcoal on Green for smaller sizes.
- **Secondary vivids (Magenta, Lime Yellow, Purple, Teal) on White** — most **fail** body-contrast; use as fills/accents with dark type on them, or large display only. Never as small type on white.
- Never rely on colour alone to convey health information, dosage, or warnings — always pair with text and icon.
- Health information, dosage, and safety copy must always meet AA at minimum, AAA where possible.

### 3.5 Typography

Haleon uses **one typeface: New Hero.** A clean, modern, highly legible grotesque — credible and human, exactly on brief for consumer health. **Five weights** carry the full system: Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, and Bold.

#### Official Font Files
The approved font files are held in the **`Font/`** folder (see the asset map in §0.1):

| File | Weight | Primary role |
|---|---|---|
| `Font/New_Hero_Light.otf` | Light | Large, airy display / oversized numerals (sparingly) |
| `Font/New_Hero_Regular.otf` | Regular | Body, captions, health information, legal |
| `Font/New_Hero_Medium.otf` | Medium | Softer headings, subheads, in-body emphasis, UI labels |
| `Font/New_Hero_SemiBold.otf` | SemiBold | Strong emphasis, stat callouts, CTA labels |
| `Font/New_Hero_Bold.otf` | Bold | H1–H5 headings, the emphasised word, authority impact |

All five are OpenType (`.otf`). Always embed the supplied files in production; never substitute a lookalike or a differently-weighted copy. Confirm licence scope (digital, print, broadcast, app, packaging, embedding) with Haleon brand/legal before new applications.

#### The Type Scale
Per the brand spec, headings use a defined scale in Bold (primary) or Medium (optional, softer) weights:

| Level | Weight | Size | Optional |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | New Hero Bold | 72px | New Hero Medium 72px |
| **H2** | New Hero Bold | 64px | New Hero Medium 64px |
| **H3** | New Hero Bold | 56px | New Hero Medium 56px |
| **H4** | New Hero Bold | 46px | New Hero Medium 46px |
| **H5** | New Hero Bold | 36px | New Hero Medium 36px |

- **Bold** headings for confident, credible impact.
- **Medium** headings (the "optional" line) for a softer, warmer, more human register — use where the tone needs empathy over authority.
- Sizes above are the digital display reference scale; scale proportionally for other media, preserving the Bold/Medium relationship and hierarchy steps.

#### Weights & Roles
- **New Hero Bold** — H1–H5 headings, the emphasised word in a headline, strong impact, key figures.
- **New Hero SemiBold** — strong emphasis, stat callouts, CTA/button labels.
- **New Hero Medium** — optional softer headings, subheads, emphasis within body, UI labels.
- **New Hero Regular** — body copy, long-form, captions, health information, legal.
- **New Hero Light** — large, airy display moments and oversized numerals only; never for body or small UI (legibility).

#### Typesetting Rules
- **Headings:** use the scale above. Sentence case for warmth; the Bold/Medium choice sets the register (authority vs empathy).
- **Body:** New Hero Regular, min 16 px digital / 9 pt print, 1.5× leading. Health and dosage copy: prioritise legibility above all — generous size and leading.
- **Emphasis:** New Hero Medium/Bold weight, Haleon Green highlight (sparingly), or Charcoal — never fake-bold, never colour that fails contrast.
- **Highlight device:** the green highlight can emphasise a single key word in a heading — one per composition.
- **Numerals:** tabular figures in dosage, data, and pricing; lining figures elsewhere. Dosage and health figures must be unambiguous.
- **Accessibility first:** never sacrifice legibility for style in any health-information context.

#### Print & Digital Type/Colour Pairings (DO)

| Surface | Type colour |
|---|---|
| White / Gray 100 | Black, Charcoal, or Haleon Green (headings) |
| Haleon Green | White (large) or Charcoal |
| Charcoal / Black | White |
| Secondary vivid fills | Black or Charcoal (never white on Lime Yellow) |
| Light photography | Black or Charcoal |
| Dark photography | White |

#### Forbidden in body copy
- New Hero at sub-16 px for body (legibility).
- Secondary vivid colours as small type on white (contrast).
- Haleon Green as small body type on white (contrast — use for headings/accents).
- All-caps for long body copy (reserved for short labels).
- Fake-bolding via CSS — use the correct New Hero weight.

#### Licensing
New Hero is a licensed typeface. The approved files are the five OpenType weights in `Font/` (`Font/New_Hero_Light/Regular/Medium/SemiBold/Bold.otf`). Confirm the licence scope (digital, print, broadcast, app, packaging, and any embedding) with Haleon brand/legal before new applications. Always embed the supplied files in production; never substitute a lookalike.

### 3.6 Imagery & Photography

Human-focused, warm, real, optimistic. Health shown through real people living their everyday lives — never clinical, never fear-based.

#### Human Health Principles
- **Real people, real moments.** Lead with the human — brushing teeth with a child, taking vitamins at breakfast, getting through a workday despite a headache. Everyday health, everyday life.
- **Warm, natural, optimistic light.** Bright and hopeful, never clinical fluorescence or moody dramatics.
- **Inclusive by design.** Represent the full diversity of the people Haleon serves — ages, ethnicities, abilities, body types, genders, family structures, health conditions. Inclusion is the purpose made visible.
- **Dignity always.** People managing health conditions are shown with dignity and agency — never as patients to be pitied or problems to be fixed.
- **Science shown humanly.** When research or science appears, show the people behind it and the people it helps — not cold lab abstraction.

#### Composition & Emotion Rules
- Bright, clean, generous space. Optimism is a lighting and framing choice.
- The green highlight and secondary palette can accent or code imagery.
- Authentic moments over staged perfection.
- Avoid: clinical/hospital coldness, fear or symptom-dwelling imagery, "before/after" suffering narratives, exclusionary or stereotyped casting, anything that makes health feel unattainable or shameful.

### 3.7 Iconography & Graphics
- **Clean, rounded, accessible** — same DNA as New Hero and the highlight device.
- 2 px stroke at 24 px; scale proportionally.
- Outline icons for UI; filled for category markers.
- Permitted motifs: the highlight/underline device, health and category glyphs (tooth, tablet, vitamin, breath, etc. — clear and universal), simple human/care symbols.
- Haleon Green or Charcoal primary; secondary palette for category coding. Always supporting, never cluttering.
- Icons used in health/dosage contexts must be unambiguous and paired with text.

### 3.8 Motion & Sonic
- Motion is warm, smooth, and clear — never frantic or clinical.
- Signature motion: the green highlight "drawing on" like a marker stroke, emphasising a key word or idea.
- Any sonic identity should feel warm, human, and reassuring; approved centrally.

## 4. Portfolio & Endorsement Guidelines

### 4.1 The House of Brands
Haleon is the parent to a portfolio of consumer-health brands built on science, innovation, and human understanding, trusted by millions of consumers globally. Each product brand has its **own identity, equity, and brand guidelines**, which lead in that brand's own advertising; Haleon endorses (§4.2).

#### Portfolio architecture: Power Brands + Local Growth Brands
- **Power Brands** — Haleon's core global brands (nine in total), prioritised for investment and consistency across markets. The flagship trio the company leads with publicly is **Sensodyne, Advil, and Centrum**.
- **Local Growth Brands** — market- or region-specific brands that support the Power Brands and address local needs.

When a specific product brand is named or shown, always use its **own approved name styling** (see the capitalisation notes below) and its **own brand guidelines** for that brand's expression. This guideline governs only the Haleon masterbrand and the endorsement relationship.

#### The six categories and their brands
Haleon's portfolio is organised into **six categories**. Representative brands per category (per haleon.com/our-brands):

| # | Category | Brands (representative) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Oral Health** *(therapeutic oral health — a leading position; toothpaste, denture care, mouthwash, toothbrushes)* | Sensodyne · parodontax · Polident |
| 2 | **Vitamins, Minerals and Supplements (VMS)** | Centrum · Emergen-C · Caltrate |
| 3 | **Respiratory** *(cold & flu, nasal decongestant, allergy)* | Otrivin · Theraflu · Flonase · Robitussin |
| 4 | **Pain Relief** *(pain relief & anti-inflammatory)* | Panadol · Voltaren · Advil · Fenbid · Contac · Grand-pa · Excedrin |

| 6 | **Therapeutic Skin Health** | Fenistil · Zovirax · Bactroban |

#### Brand-name styling (respect exact casing)
Product-brand names are trademarks — reproduce each exactly as the brand sets it, never "normalised":
- **parodontax** — always lowercase "p".
- **Grand-pa** — capital "G", hyphen, lowercase "pa".
- **Emergen-C**, **Grand-pa**, **Benefiber**, **Robitussin**, etc. — use the brand's own logo/wordmark artwork where a logo is shown; in running text, match the brand's official casing.
- **Tums / TUMS** — "Tums" in running text; the logo wordmark may appear in caps. Follow the brand's own guidelines.
- Never apply the Haleon green "E" device or the Haleon masterbrand system to any product-brand name or logo (§3.1, §4.2).

### 4.2 The Endorsement Relationship
- In **product advertising**, the product brand is the hero. Haleon endorses — as a credibility signal and corporate signature — via the approved endorsement lockup, never overshadowing the product brand.
- In **corporate, purpose, employer, investor, and inclusivity contexts**, the Haleon masterbrand leads.
- The Haleon green highlight and masterbrand system are **not** imposed on product-brand identities. Product brands keep their own colours, logos, and equity.

### 4.3 When Haleon Leads vs Endorses

| Context | Who leads |
|---|---|
| Product advertising (a specific product) | Product brand leads; Haleon endorses |
| Corporate / purpose communications | Haleon masterbrand leads |
| Employer / recruitment | Haleon masterbrand leads |
| Investor / ESG / annual report | Haleon masterbrand leads |
| Health inclusivity / social impact | Haleon masterbrand leads (may feature product brands) |
| Retailer / trade (portfolio) | Haleon masterbrand leads, product brands featured |

### 4.4 Consistency
- The Haleon masterbrand system (wordmark, green highlight, New Hero, palette) is consistent across all corporate/masterbrand touchpoints.
- Endorsement lockups are consistent across the portfolio.
- Product-brand identities remain distinct and are never diluted by the masterbrand.

## 10. Compliance & Brand Safety

> Consumer health is among the most heavily regulated advertising categories in the world. This section is the operating licence for the brand, not optional guidance.

### 10.1 Regulatory & Legal Approvals
- **All health-related communications** require clearance by Haleon Regulatory/Medical and Legal before publication.
- **Every health claim** must be substantiated, compliant with the relevant market's regulations, and approved before use.
- **Product status governs everything:** whether a product is a medicine/OTC, medical device, food supplement, cosmetic, or general product determines what may be said and what disclaimers are required — per market.
- Corporate, investor, and ESG communications comply with listing/securities disclosure rules (Haleon is publicly listed).

### 10.2 Health Claims — Hard Rules
- **Substantiate every claim.** No efficacy, benefit, "science-backed," or comparative claim without approved evidence on file.
- **Never overstate or imply cures.** Stay within the approved, evidenced claim for the specific product and market.
- **Never diagnose** or position a product as a substitute for professional medical advice where inappropriate; include "speak to your doctor/pharmacist" guidance where required.
- **Carry mandatory safety information, dosage, warnings, and disclaimers** as required by market and product status — never obscured, always legible and accessible.
- **"Science-led" / "trusted science"** masterbrand language must be defensible and must not imply specific unproven product claims.
- **Comply with the strictest applicable rule** for any cross-market asset.

### 10.3 Vulnerable Audiences & Responsible Marketing
- Never target or exploit vulnerable audiences; take special care with children's health, chronic conditions, and mental health.
- No fear-based, shame-based, or anxiety-inducing health marketing.
- Represent health conditions accurately and with dignity; never sensationalise.

### 10.4 Imagery & Claims Integrity
- Imagery must not misrepresent a product, its results, or a health condition.
- "Results" depictions must reflect realistic, substantiated outcomes with disclosures where required.
- Product images in any context must match the actual product and its market status.

### 10.5 Inclusivity, Accessibility & Data
- Inclusive representation is authentic, not performative; accessibility meets or exceeds AA (AAA for health info).
- Health-data and privacy communications comply with applicable data-protection law (GDPR and market equivalents); health data is sensitive data and handled accordingly.

### 10.6 Portfolio & Endorsement Integrity
- Product-brand claims live under that brand's guidelines and regulatory approvals; the masterbrand never introduces unapproved claims into product work.
- The Haleon endorsement never implies a claim the endorsed product hasn't substantiated.

### 10.7 Confidentiality
Internal use only across the Haleon system: employees, agencies, and approved partners. Not for external distribution without approval.

### 11.2 Campaign Generation Rules

Any generated masterbrand campaign **must**:

1. **Map to one masterbrand tier** — Purpose & Brand / Corporate & Reputation / Endorsement & Portfolio (45/30/25).
2. **Respect architecture** — product brand leads in product advertising; Haleon leads in corporate/purpose/employer/investor/inclusivity.
3. **Anchor on one Health Truth** — everyday, human, empowering.
4. **Pair evidence with empathy** — science-with-humanity in every message.
5. **Use the Haleon wordmark + green highlight** correctly; never apply the highlight to product-brand logos.
6. **Build white-dominant, accessible** layouts; Haleon Green as signature accent; secondary palette sparingly (max two).
7. **Use New Hero** per the heading scale (Bold for authority, Medium for empathy); Regular for body; never below 16 px body.
8. **Keep copy clear, plain, inclusive, empowering** — no fear, no shame, no jargon.
9. **Reserve the purpose line** for masterbrand moments; never on product advertising.
10. **CTA clear and empowering, ≤3 words.**
11. **Meet accessibility** — AA minimum, AAA for health info; never colour-only for health status/dosage/warnings.
12. **NEVER output a health claim, efficacy/comparative statement, dosage, or safety statement** without a pre-approved, substantiated, market-specific value — block and route to regulatory/medical/legal.
13. **NEVER imply cures, diagnose, or replace professional advice** inappropriately.
14. **Represent people inclusively and with dignity;** never fear- or shame-based; special care for children, chronic conditions, mental health.
15. **Multilingual + market-regulation aware** — public work adapted natively and re-cleared per market.

