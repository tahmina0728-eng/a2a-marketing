# Boozt Brand Guidelines

**Enterprise Brand & Campaign System**
*Version 2.0 — Energy Drinks. Built for marketing teams, agencies, and AI-driven campaign generation systems.*

> **Brand context.** Boozt is an energy drink brand. We make canned energy drinks that deliver clean, fast, sustained energy — for athletes, students, gamers, festival-goers, and anyone who needs to switch on. The stylised spelling isn't a typo; it's the brand. We don't boost. We Boozt.

> **Document purpose.** A single source of truth so any team — internal, agency, or automated — can produce on-brand work without additional guidance.

---

## 1. Brand Overview

### 1.1 Brand Promise
**Pure energy. Zero limits.**

The promise every campaign, pack, and piece of copy must pass: does this deliver — or visibly show — instant energy, performance, and the drive to push further?

### 1.2 Mission & Vision
- **Mission:** To fuel every peak moment — the workout, the all-nighter, the festival set, the deadline — with energy that actually delivers.
- **Vision:** To own "energy" as a brand the way Red Bull owns "wings" — brand-as-verb, instant recognition, instant action.

### 1.3 Values
- **Performance** — real energy for real effort.
- **Fast** — energy in seconds, sustained for hours.
- **Bold** — the brand, the spelling, the attitude. We don't apologise for any of it.
- **Inclusive** — athletes, students, gamers, creatives — energy is a universal need.
- **Playful** — we take performance seriously. We don't take ourselves seriously.

### 1.4 Brand Story (positioning narrative)
"Boost" is everywhere. Supplements. Apps. Career advice. It's the most overused promise in marketing — and the most underdelivered. We changed the spelling because we needed the brand to do what every other "boost" couldn't: stand out, stick, and actually deliver at the can. Boozt is what happens when you stop accepting tired as the default.

### 1.5 Core Brand Promise (Operational)
The promise broken into three commitments:
1. **Instant energy** — fast-acting, feel-it-in-minutes uplift.
2. **Sustained performance** — not a crash, a wave. Energy that works with you, not against you.
3. **Zero compromise** — full flavour, real energy, no excuses. Whether Zero Sugar, Sport Hydration, or Classic.

### 1.6 Product Range
- **Original Energy** — the signature Boozt. Full flavour, full energy. The everyday can.
- **Zero Sugar** — same energy, zero sugar. Clean fuel for the focused.
- **Sport Hydration** — electrolytes + energy. Built for the gym and the pitch.
- **Tropical Blast** — mango-passion punch energy. Festival and beach season hero.
- **Arctic Mint** — ice-cool mint energy. Late-night sessions and graveyard shift fuel.
- **Classic** — the heritage formula. For when you want the original.

## 2. Brand Personality & Voice

### 2.1 Brand Archetype
**Magician** primary, **Jester** secondary. We make instant transformation happen — from tired to switched-on — and we have fun while doing it.

### 2.2 Personality Attributes
- **Punchy**
- **Confident**
- **Playful**
- **Fast**
- **Modern**

### 2.3 Tone of Voice
Consistent across can, social, ads, app:
- **Direct** — say it, don't dance around it.
- **Witty** — humour earned through brand-name play, not gags.
- **Energetic** — the copy moves at the speed of the brand.
- **Self-aware** — we know "Boozt" is a misspelling. That's the point.

### 2.4 Voice Principle
**Speak like you've got somewhere to be.**

Not breathless, not rushed — energetic. Every line moves. Every word earns its place. If a sentence drags, it's not Boozt.

### 2.5 Creative Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Energy is visible** | Show the can in the moment — mid-crack, condensation, the pour, the first sip face. The energy is in the image. |
| **Performance is the system** | Motion in image, italics in type, brand name doing verb-work in copy. Every element carries forward momentum. |
| **Loud, not noisy** | Bold colour, confident scale, italic emphasis — disciplined. Three things shouting is loud. Ten things shouting is noise. |

### 2.6 The Brand-As-Verb Device

Boozt is a verb. It's the brand's most distinctive linguistic asset.

**Conjugation:**
- Verb form: *to boozt* — to energise, to switch on, to fuel up.
- Imperative: *boozt it, boozt up, get a Boozt.*
- Past: *boozted* — "I boozted before the gym."
- Adjective: *boozted* — switched on, energised, ready.

**Approved usage patterns:**
- "Get a Boozt." (tagline / sign-off)
- "Boozt your session."
- "Boozt your game."
- "Boozted in 3 seconds."
- "Need a Boozt?"

**Capitalisation rule:** "Boozt" is always capitalised as a proper noun. The verb form *boozt* / *boozted* is lowercase when used as an action.

**Don't overdo it.** One brand-as-verb moment per asset. Two if the joke earns it. Three is cringe.

### 2.7 Energy Truths

We organise creative around small, real moments from the energy journey. A great Energy Truth is:
- **Specific** — a real moment, not a generic insight.
- **Visual** — it shows up as an image, not just a line of copy.
- **Fast** — it lives in 3 seconds or less.

Examples:
- "That first sip when you need to switch on — that's a Boozt moment."
- "Peak performance isn't a personality — it's a decision you make every day."
- "The can that turns 'I can't' into 'watch me'."
- "Zero limits. Pure energy. One can."
- "The 3-second crack before you go all in."
- "When everyone else is fading — you're just getting started."

### 2.8 Do's and Don'ts of Communication

**DO**
- Lead with the energy moment — the can, the crack, the first sip.
- Use the brand name as a verb. Sparingly. Confidently.
- Keep sentences short. One idea per line.
- Show athletes, students, gamers, festival-goers in real peak-performance moments.
- Talk like a friend who already knows you're capable.

**DON'T**
- Use "tired" or "exhausted" language that frames the consumer as broken.
- Use "boost" anywhere in copy. The whole point of the spelling is consistency.
- Frame energy as a crutch — it's a choice, not a desperation.
- Use health or medical claims without regulatory clearance.
- Over-italicise. Italics are display. Body is upright.

### 2.9 Example Messaging
- "Get a Boozt."
- "Switch on."
- "Zero limits. One can."
- "Energy, but louder."
- "Peak performance. Every time."
- "The can that goes hard."
- "Boozt your game."
- "From flat to full throttle."

## 3. Visual Identity System

### 3.1 Logo — The Boozt Tag

The Boozt identity is a horizontal pill ("the Tag") in a brand colour, with the BOOZT wordmark in white italic uppercase inside it. Around the Tag, **two loose hand-drawn outline strokes** orbit at the edges — the brand's signature **Energy Strokes**.

#### The Three Construction Rules (non-negotiable)
1. **Always a horizontal pill shape (stadium / lozenge)** — fully rounded ends, parallel top and bottom edges.
2. **Wordmark always white, always italic uppercase**, centred and optically balanced inside the Tag.
3. **Energy Strokes always present** — two loose hand-drawn outline strokes orbiting the Tag's edge.

### 3.4 Tagline — "Get a Boozt."

The brand's signature sign-off. Three words, one period, full stop.

- **Use with discipline.** It's a sign-off, not a filler.
- **Recommended use:** TVC end cards, hero campaign films, master packaging back panels, social bumpers.
- **Set in:** Rubik 700 Italic, sentence case, with the period.

#### Approved tagline variations (sparingly)
- "Get a Boozt." (default)
- "Need a Boozt?" (question hook for retargeting)
- "Boozt up." (imperative shorthand for short-form social)

### 3.5 Colour Palette

#### Master Palette (Brand Identity)

| Colour | Role | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| **Midnight** | The Tag fill — master brand surface | **#0E105E** |
| **Boozt Blue** | Energy Stroke primary / accent / CTA | **#0086FE** |
| **Sky** | Secondary energy / lighter Stroke / gradient end | **#00BFFE** |
| **White** | Wordmark, body surface, type on Midnight | **#FFFFFF** |

#### Range Palette (Sub-Brand Lines)

| Range | Tag Fill | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| **Original Energy** | Midnight #0E105E | Confidence, pure energy |
| **Zero Sugar** | Electric Green #1DB954 | Clean, performance-focused |
| **Sport Hydration** | Cobalt #0047AB | Athletic, precision |
| **Tropical Blast** | Mango #FF6B2B | Fun, festival season |
| **Arctic Mint** | Ice Blue #6EE7F7 | Cool, late-night sessions |

### 3.6 Typography

Boozt uses **one typeface, two voices**: Rubik 700 Italic carries the brand at scale; Rubik upright carries it at reading distance.

#### Rubik 700 Italic — Display
- **Use only for type 24 px / 14 pt and larger.**
- **Always italic, always 700 weight.**
- Sentence case for editorial headlines; UPPERCASE for short, punch headlines (≤4 words).

#### Rubik (Variable, Upright) — Body & UI
- **Rubik Medium** — subheads, navigation, captions.
- **Rubik Regular** — body copy, instructions, captions.

### 3.7 Imagery & Photography

#### Energy Photography Principles

- **Performance is the subject.** Every hero shot must visibly demonstrate energy, focus, or peak-moment intensity.
- **Show the can in action.** Condensation droplets, the crack of the ring pull, the pour, mid-sip — the can is the hero.
- **Motion-friendly framing.** Show athletes mid-sprint, gamers leaning in, festival-goers mid-jump. Stillness is the enemy of an energy brand.
- **Authentic intensity.** No faked exertion. Shoot real athletes, real moments, real energy.

#### Lifestyle Photography Principles

- **Pre-performance energy.** The brand lives in the moment before you go all in. That's the emotional space.
- **Real spaces, peak moments.** A real gym, a real stadium, a real festival. Mid-action, never staged perfection.
- **Inclusive casting.** Range across ages (16–35 core), ethnicities, genders, body types. Athletes, students, gamers, creatives.
- **The can as the anchor.** The Boozt can appears in hand, mid-sip, or as a prop — never hidden or secondary.

## 4. Audience Segmentation

Boozt is a performance-led brand. The core audience is **16–34**, social-first, action-oriented.

### 4.1 The Athlete
- **Insight:** Trains hard. Needs real fuel, not sugar crashes.
- **Hooks:** Sport Hydration range, pre-workout moments, performance recovery.
- **Tone:** Direct, evidence-led. "The can built for what you're actually doing."

### 4.2 The Student
- **Insight:** Late nights, deadlines, the library at 2am.
- **Hooks:** Zero Sugar, Arctic Mint, study-session energy.
- **Tone:** Relatable, punchy. "Lectures at 9. Studied at 3. Boozt helped."

### 4.3 The Gamer
- **Insight:** Endurance matters. Hours-long sessions need sustained focus, not spikes.
- **Hooks:** Original Energy, Zero Sugar, long-session focus.
- **Tone:** Community-fluent, self-aware. "GG. Get a Boozt."

### 4.4 The Festival-Goer
- **Insight:** Energy that holds through a 3-day weekend.
- **Hooks:** Tropical Blast, festival partnerships, OOH activations.
- **Tone:** Hype, inclusive, fun. "The set doesn't stop. Neither do you."

### 4.5 The Young Professional
- **Insight:** Early mornings, long commutes, side hustles after hours.
- **Hooks:** Original Energy, Classic, everyday carry.
- **Tone:** Ambitious, efficient. "Peak performance isn't a personality — it's a decision."

## 5. Messaging & Copywriting Framework

### 5.1 Messaging Pillars

Every campaign ladders up to one or more:

1. **Instant Energy** — fast-acting, feel-it uplift.
2. **Brand-As-Verb** — owning "boozt" as a word.
3. **Peak Performance** — energy that matches your ambition.
4. **Zero Limits** — the emotional payoff of choosing Boozt.

### 5.2 Language Patterns
- **Short sentences.** Headlines under 8 words.
- **Active verbs.** Switch, boozt, push, go, hit, fuel, crack. Avoid linking verbs.
- **Specific over generic.** "Zero sugar. Full energy." beats "great taste."
- **Brand-as-verb.** Once per piece, twice max. Earn it.

### 5.3 Emotional Triggers
- **Confidence** (the moment before you go all in)
- **Speed** (fast-acting energy)
- **Self-determination** (you chose to show up — Boozt chose to help)
- **Belonging** (your crew, your session, your moment)
- **Play** (boozt as a fun verb, as a wink)

### 8.2 Campaign Generation Rules

Any generated campaign **must**:

1. **Be for energy drinks** — never generate copy about hair care, hair volume, or styling products.
2. **Hero the can** — the Boozt can (condensation, energy, performance context) is the primary visual.
3. **Anchor on one Energy Truth** — specific, visual, fast (≤3 seconds to read).
4. **Use performance audience** — athletes, students, gamers, festival-goers, young professionals.
5. **No hair references** — any copy or concept referencing hair, volume (for hair), or styling is categorically off-brand.
6. **Apply the right Tag colour:**
   - Master Midnight for core brand and master campaigns.
   - Range colour (Zero Sugar green, Sport Hydration cobalt, etc.) when range-led.
7. **Use Rubik 700 Italic for display, Rubik upright for body.**
8. **Brand-as-verb used max twice** per asset.
9. **CTA verb-led, ≤3 words.**
10. **Pass the "speak like you've got somewhere to be" voice test.**
11. **No forbidden language** — never "boost", "hair", "volume (for hair)", "lift (for hair)".

### 8.3 Generation Workflow (Agent Sequence)

```
1. CLASSIFY input → {tier, campaign_type, audience_segment, channel, market, product_variant}
2. RETRIEVE Energy Truth aligned to audience + occasion
3. DETERMINE Tag colour:
     - master Midnight if core/master campaign
     - range colour if variant-led (Zero Sugar, Sport Hydration, etc.)
4. DRAFT hero message (≤8 words, Rubik 700 Italic, "somewhere to be" voice)
5. DECIDE brand-as-verb usage (0, 1, or 2 max per asset)
6. DRAFT supporting message (1–2 lines, Rubik Medium upright)
7. SELECT visual system: can hero, performance moment, energy context
8. VALIDATE: no hair care references anywhere in copy or image brief
9. VALIDATE forbidden-term filter on copy
10. ASSEMBLE per channel template
11. VALIDATE against 8.1 + 8.2 → flag every violation
```
