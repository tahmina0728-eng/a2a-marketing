## Brand Voice
Warm, upbeat, inclusive. We speak to everyone — families, individuals, late-night cravings.
Tone: Friendly but confident. Never preachy. Always optimistic.

## Visual Identity
- Golden Arches (M logo) must appear on all brand communications
- Red and yellow are primary brand colours — never swap or substitute
- Clean, bold typography using Speedee font
- Food photography must be bright, fresh, appetising — never dark or moody

## Product Representation Standards
- All food must be shown at its best — fresh, hot, accurate to actual product
- No misleading portion sizes or unrealistic presentations
- Products must match current menu items exactly

## Channel-Specific Rules
### Instagram / Social
- Bright, thumb-stopping visuals
- Short copy — lead with emotion, not features
- Always include a clear CTA

### OOH / Outdoor
- 3-second rule: message must land in 3 seconds
- Minimal text — logo + hero image + tagline maximum
- High contrast for legibility at distance

### TikTok / Video
- Hook in first 2 seconds
- Sound-on and sound-off versions required
- Authentic, lo-fi aesthetic encouraged

## Key Messages
- Iconic products people love and crave
- Everyday moments made special
- Value without compromise
- Community and belonging

## Restricted Content
- No political messaging
- No health claims beyond regulatory-approved statements
- No competitor comparisons
- No imagery of unhappy or distressed people
