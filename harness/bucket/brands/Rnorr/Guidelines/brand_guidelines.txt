# Rnorr Brand Guidelines

**Enterprise Brand & Campaign System**
*Version 1.0 — Built for marketing teams, agencies, and AI-driven campaign generation systems.*

> **Brand context.** Rnorr is a flavor-enhancer brand specialising in bouillons, stock cubes, stock pots, and soups. We exist to help home cooks build real depth of flavor into everyday meals — quickly, confidently, and without compromise.

> **Document purpose.** A single source of truth so that any team — internal, agency, or automated — can produce on-brand work without additional guidance.

---

## 1. Brand Overview

### 1.1 Brand Promise
**Making real flavor easy for every home cook.**

This promise is the test every campaign, pack design, and piece of copy must pass: does this help someone build genuine depth of flavor, more easily, in their own kitchen?

### 1.2 Mission & Vision
- **Mission:** To give every home cook the confidence and the shortcut to cook with real flavor — no matter their skill, schedule, or kitchen.
- **Vision:** A world where a great-tasting home-cooked meal is never out of reach.

### 1.3 Values
- **Flavor First** — every product, recipe, and campaign starts with taste.
- **Real Ingredients** — we source recognisable, kitchen-honest ingredients and say so plainly.
- **Generous** — we share knowledge, recipes, and shortcuts freely.
- **Inclusive** — we cook for every kitchen, every diet, every culture we serve.
- **Practical** — we respect time. We never make cooking harder than it needs to be.

### 1.4 Brand Story (positioning narrative)
Most kitchens have one thing in common: a quiet shelf where the everyday essentials live. Rnorr is built for that shelf. We're the warm green pillow tucked next to the salt — the small thing that turns a weeknight pan into something worth sitting down for. We don't replace home cooking. We make it taste like more time was spent on it than really was.

### 1.5 Core Brand Promise (Operational)
The promise broken into three commitments:
1. **Real flavor** — depth that tastes built, not bottled.
2. **Easy** — minimum effort, minimum friction, minimum cleanup.
3. **For every home cook** — no skill gatekeeping, no aspirational gloss, no "chef" attitude.

### 1.6 Global vs Local Positioning
Rnorr operates as a **globally consistent masterbrand with deep local expression.** Cuisine is cultural — our identity, voice, and visual system stay constant; our flavors, recipes, talent, and product range adapt to every market we serve. See Section 9.

## 2. Brand Personality & Voice

### 2.1 Brand Archetype
**Caregiver**, with a secondary **Sage** lean. We nurture (we feed people) and we teach (we know flavor). Both come through warmth, never authority. We are the trusted friend in the kitchen, not the chef on the pedestal.

### 2.2 Personality Attributes
- **Warm**
- **Generous**
- **Knowing** (without being know-it-all)
- **Practical**
- **Encouraging**

### 2.3 Tone of Voice
Consistent across packaging, social, recipes, and ads:
- **Confident** — we know flavor.
- **Inviting** — every reader is welcome at the stove.
- **Plain-spoken** — clear, real-kitchen language.
- **Quietly witty** — humour earned, not forced.

### 2.4 Voice Principle
**Talk like the friend who actually cooks.**

Not the celebrity chef, not the food scientist, not the brand. The friend who texts you "add a stock pot, thank me later." Every line should pass the friend-in-the-kitchen test.

### 2.5 Creative Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Flavor is the hero** | The product is in service of the meal, not the other way around. Show the food. Show the depth. Talk about taste before convenience. |
| **Confident simplicity** | The kitchen doesn't need theatre. Clean compositions, generous space, real ingredients, no garnish for garnish's sake. |
| **Steam, not smoke** | Warmth, not drama. Our brand feels like a slow simmer — inviting, fragrant, unhurried — not a sizzling spectacle. |

### 2.6 Kitchen Truths
We organise creative around small, universally recognisable truths from real kitchens. A great Kitchen Truth is:
- **Real** — a thing that actually happens in home kitchens (the lid that won't quite fit, the recipe missing one ingredient at 7pm).
- **Shared** — recognisable across cultures and skill levels.
- **Resolved** — Rnorr quietly makes it better. The truth lands first; the product earns its place second.

Examples:
- "The reason the soup tastes like it took all day."
- "When the recipe says 'rich broth' and you've got tap water and twelve minutes."
- "The thing that's been on the shelf the whole time."

### 2.7 Do's and Don'ts of Communication

**DO**
- Lead with flavor. Describe taste in real, sensory terms (rich, deep, savoury, mellow, golden).
- Talk about cooking like it's a real act done by real people, not a performance.
- Earn humour through specificity ("the Tuesday pasta," not "weeknight magic").
- Use generous, encouraging language — confidence is a gift we hand over.
- Show the meal more than the pack.

**DON'T**
- Use chef-snob language ("artisanal," "curated," "elevated").
- Stage perfection. Real plates have crumbs.
- Talk down to home cooks or imply they were doing it wrong.
- Lean on "magic" or "secret" as flavor explanations — name what's in it.
- Overuse the tagline. It's a sign-off, not a closer for everything.

### 2.8 Example Messaging
- "Real flavor. Less work. Same kitchen."
- "Tastes like Sunday. On a Tuesday."
- "The shortcut that tastes like time."
- "Worth the spoon you'll lick clean."
- **Tagline:** "Tastes like time." — used as a sign-off, not a workhorse line.

## 3. Visual Identity System

### 3.1 Logo — The Rnorr Wordmark

The Rnorr identity is a custom white italic script set inside an asymmetric green pillow.

#### The Three Construction Rules (non-negotiable)
1. **Always white script wordmark on Rnorr Green.** The wordmark colour and background colour are locked.
2. **Always inside the Pillow.** The wordmark is never used outside the green pillow container in primary brand applications.
3. **Asymmetric corner rounding preserved.** Top-left and bottom-right corners are heavily rounded; top-right and bottom-left are subtly rounded. This asymmetry is the silhouette.

#### Naming
Refer to the brand mark in copy as **"the Rnorr Pillow"** (the container) or **"the Rnorr wordmark"** (the script). Never abbreviate to "the logo" in formal brand documentation.

#### Clear Space
- Clearspace equals the **height of the lowercase "n"** in the wordmark, applied on all four sides outside the Pillow.
- Minimum size: **Print 8 mm height** (Pillow) / **Digital 32 px height** (Pillow).

#### Trademark
The mark requires ® on packaging, advertising, and corporate communications. Not required on internal-only or social organic where space is constrained.

#### Approval
Treated or non-standard logo uses (limited-edition crops, partnership co-locks, motion treatments) require approval from **Global Brand + Legal IP** at concept and pre-final stages.

#### Permitted Techniques
- **Scale variation** — the Pillow can flex large or small within minimum-size rules.
- **Cropping** — for OOH and packaging "moments" where the Pillow shape extends beyond the canvas. Crops must always reveal the full wordmark; never crop the wordmark itself.
- **Motion** — the Pillow can subtly pulse, ladle in, or sit under steam in animation. Wordmark stays static and legible.
- **Embossing/debossing** — on premium pack variants, the Pillow can be embossed in metallic green foil. Wordmark remains in true white.

#### DO
- Place the Pillow as the primary brand anchor on every asset.
- Use the master green-and-white version wherever contrast allows.
- Use the all-white "knockout" version on dark or photographic backgrounds.

#### DO NOT
- Change the green to any other colour.
- Outline, drop-shadow, gradient, or 3D the wordmark.
- Rotate the Pillow more than ±5° (any animation returns to upright).
- Stretch, skew, or distort the Pillow's asymmetric silhouette.
- Place the wordmark outside the Pillow.
- Use the wordmark as a letter substitute in other words.
- Use older versions of the mark (sunset on retirement date).
- Place over busy photography that compromises legibility.

### 3.2 The Pillow — Standalone Container

The asymmetric green pillow can be used as a **standalone brand container** without the wordmark, for use as:
- A visual frame for recipes, tips, and price flags.
- A graphic hold for product imagery on dark, photographic, or busy backgrounds.
- A repeating system element in packaging and digital UI.

When used standalone (no wordmark), the Pillow must:
- Remain in **Rnorr Green** (or knockout white on dark surfaces).
- Maintain its asymmetric corner radius proportions.
- Carry the Rnorr wordmark somewhere else on the asset (clear secondary placement).

### 3.3 Tagline — "Tastes like time."

A custom-set tagline used to close TVCs, packaging back panels, and signature brand films.

- **Use with discipline.** Overuse drains its meaning. Showing flavor is more powerful than saying it.
- **Recommended use:** end cards, hero brand campaigns, master pack designs.
- **Set in:** Rubik Medium Italic, sentence case, always with the period.
- **Colour:** Rnorr Green on White or light photography, White on Rnorr Green / Rnorr Yellow / dark photography.
- **Clearspace:** equal to the cap-height of the typeset tagline.
- **Minimum size:** Print 4 mm / Digital 14 px.
- **Localisation:** market-approved translation only. Confirm with local Legal before public use.

### 3.4 Colour Palette

The Rnorr palette is **deliberately tight: three colours.** Green for brand, Yellow for warmth and appetite, White for breathing room. Discipline here is the system — adding colour outside this trio dilutes recognition.

#### Brand Palette (Master)

| Colour | PMS (approx.) | CMYK | RGB | HEX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rnorr Green** | 356 C | 100.0.78.47 | 0.134.65 | **#008641** |
| **Rnorr Yellow** | 109 C | 0.13.100.0 | 255.222.0 | **#FFDE00** |
| **White** | — | 0.0.0.0 | 255.255.255 | **#FFFFFF** |

- **Rnorr Green** is the brand anchor — the colour of the Pillow, the colour the brand is known by. Dominant on every primary brand expression.
- **Rnorr Yellow** is the appetite accent — golden broth, the warmth on the surface of a pot, the spark of flavor. Used to draw the eye, highlight value, and signal moments of generosity.
- **White** is the breathing-room base. Used as the primary background on long-form digital, packaging real-estate, and clean recipe content.

#### Functional Palette (Limited Use)

| Colour | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Black** | #000000 | Legal type and fine print only |
| **Charcoal** | #1A1A1A | Body type on White surfaces |
| **Soft Gray** | #E8E8E8 | UI dividers, recipe-card backgrounds, subtle separators |

The functional palette is the **only** acceptable extension of the master palette. No additional brand colours, no decorative tints, no gradient variants of the three.

#### Extended Palette (Restricted)
Sub-brand or variant-specific palettes (e.g., a premium bone-broth line, a chef collaboration) **may** request additional colours, but require Global Brand approval and must never appear in master-brand communications. Extended colours never appear in core brand assets, Always-on campaigns, or master packaging architecture.

#### Colour Proportion Rules
- **Rnorr Green dominant**, anchored by the Pillow.
- **White as the primary background** for long-form and digital surfaces.
- **Rnorr Yellow as accent, not base** — used to highlight a value moment, a CTA, a Pillow contrast pairing, or a flavor cue. Generous use is permitted on Brand Moments and OOH where appetite needs to land hard.
- Generous breathing room. Negative space is brand-active — the white between things does work.

#### The Green–Yellow Relationship
The Rnorr Green / Rnorr Yellow pairing is the brand's most ownable visual signature. When used together they should:
- **Sit in clear blocks**, not blend. No gradients. No tints. Sharp edges.
- **Carry strong proportion logic** — typically Green as the base with a Yellow accent panel, or Yellow as a value field with the Green Pillow anchoring it.
- **Never split 50/50** in the same composition — one always leads. Equal-weight pairings flatten the system.

#### Engagement-Based Colour Volume

| Engagement Length | Description | Brand Volume | Product Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Glance** (≤3s) | OOH, flash digital, end cards | 10 | 1 |
| **Browse** (1–2 min) | Social, banner, pack on shelf | 6 | 5 |
| **Cook** (2+ min) | Recipe page, in-app, packaging back panel | 2 | 10 |

#### Colour DO / DO NOT
- **DO** lead with Rnorr Green and the Pillow as the brand anchor.
- **DO** use White as the primary breathing-room surface on long-form digital and packaging panels.
- **DO** use Rnorr Yellow to draw the eye — value flags, key callouts, Brand Moment fields, appetite-led OOH.
- **DO** keep the Green–Yellow relationship in clear blocks, never blended.
- **DO NOT** introduce greens, yellows, or any other colours outside the master palette.
- **DO NOT** tint, gradient, or fade the brand colours.
- **DO NOT** use Rnorr Yellow as body text — fails contrast on White at every size.
- **DO NOT** use Rnorr Green type at small digital sizes (under 18 px).
- **DO NOT** split Green and Yellow 50/50 in the same composition.

#### Accessibility
- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum for all body copy: 4.5:1 contrast.
- **White on Rnorr Green** — passes AA at all sizes. Primary type pairing for green surfaces.
- **Charcoal on White** — passes AAA. Primary body-text pairing.
- **Charcoal on Rnorr Yellow** — passes AA at all sizes. Use for type on yellow.
- **Rnorr Green on White** — passes AA for body and AAA for large text. Approved for headlines.
- **White on Rnorr Yellow** — **fails contrast.** Never use for type.
- **Rnorr Yellow on White** — **fails contrast.** Never use for type.
- Never rely on colour alone to convey product variant or state — always pair with a name, icon, or label.

### 3.5 Typography

Rnorr uses a **two-font system**. Antonio carries the brand's voice at scale; Rubik carries it at reading distance. Both are variable fonts on a weight axis, giving us full latitude across the entire range without licensing additional weights.

#### Antonio — Display
A tall, narrow, modern sans for headlines, hero callouts, packaging variant names, and any moment where the brand needs to **assert appetite at a glance.** Antonio is what Rnorr looks like across a room.

- **Antonio Bold** — primary display weight. Hero headlines, OOH, packaging variant names, price flags.
- **Antonio Semibold** — secondary display, subheads on Brand Moment assets, recipe-card titles where extra impact is needed.
- **Antonio Regular** — large-scale supporting type (rare; use only where Antonio Bold would overpower).
- **Use only for type 24 px / 14 pt and larger.** Antonio is not a body font.

#### Rubik — Body & UI
A friendly, slightly rounded humanist sans for body, UI, recipe instructions, microcopy, and anything that needs to be read carefully. Rubik is what Rnorr sounds like up close — warm, plain-spoken, easy to live with.

- **Rubik Medium** — subheads, recipe titles at smaller scales, navigation, emphasis within body.
- **Rubik Regular** — body copy, recipe steps, packaging instructions, captions.
- **Rubik Light** — long-form editorial body where Regular would feel heavy.
- **Rubik Medium Italic** — the tagline, sensory descriptors ("rich," "golden," "slow"), ingredient pull-quotes.

System fallback (where the brand fonts cannot be embedded): Arial Narrow → Impact → sans-serif (for Antonio); -apple-system → Inter → sans-serif (for Rubik).

#### When to use which

| Element | Font | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Hero headline (TVC, OOH, packaging) | Antonio | Bold |
| Brand Moment supers | Antonio | Bold |
| Packaging variant name | Antonio | Bold |
| Price flag / value callout | Antonio | Bold |
| Subhead (small to mid scale) | Rubik | Medium |
| Body / recipe step / caption | Rubik | Regular |
| Long-form editorial body | Rubik | Light |
| Tagline | Rubik | Medium Italic |
| Sensory descriptor inside copy | Rubik | Italic (any weight) |
| Legal / back-of-pack fine print | Rubik | Regular at 7–8 pt |

#### Typesetting Rules
- **Headlines:** max 8 words. Sentence case. Antonio Bold. Tight leading (1.0–1.1×).
- **Subheads:** Rubik Medium. 1.2× leading.
- **Body:** Rubik Regular at minimum 16 px digital / 9 pt print. 1.5× leading for cooking-context copy (recipes, steps) — readability while at the stove matters.
- **Italics:** sensory descriptors and tagline only. Don't use italics for general emphasis (use Rubik Medium for emphasis).
- **Numerals:** tabular figures in recipes, ingredient lists, and price flags. Lining figures elsewhere.
- **Pairing rhythm:** Antonio's vertical narrow geometry contrasts with Rubik's open roundness — the rhythm between the two is the brand's typographic signature. **Always pair them.** Avoid all-Antonio or all-Rubik layouts on hero assets.

#### Print Type/Colour Pairings (DO)

| Surface | Type colour |
|---|---|
| White | Charcoal, Rnorr Green, or Black |
| Rnorr Green | White |
| Rnorr Yellow | Charcoal or Rnorr Green |
| Light photography | Charcoal or Rnorr Green |
| Dark photography | White or Rnorr Yellow |

#### Digital Type/Colour Pairings (DO)

| Surface | Type colour |
|---|---|
| White | Charcoal (body), Rnorr Green (headline) |
| Rnorr Green | White |
| Rnorr Yellow | Charcoal |
| Photography (light) | Charcoal |
| Photography (dark) | White |

#### Forbidden in digital body copy
- Rnorr Green type at sizes under 18 px (legibility).
- Rnorr Yellow type on White (insufficient contrast).
- White type on Rnorr Yellow (insufficient contrast).
- Antonio for body copy at any size (it's a display face).
- All-caps body copy (reserved for legal and pack labels).

#### Licensing
Antonio (Google Fonts, OFL) and Rubik (Google Fonts, OFL) are both Open Font License typefaces — free for commercial use across all Rnorr applications including digital, print, broadcast, app, and packaging. No additional licence required.

If a third-party font is ever proposed (for a Brand Moment, partnership, or limited edition), Legal approval is required and the licence must explicitly cover the intended application.

### 3.6 Imagery & Photography

#### Product / Food Photography Principles

- **Hero the dish, not the pack.** Most assets lead with the finished meal. Pack appears as a supporting witness — never as the headline.
- **Steam, gloss, drip.** Real cooking signals. Not glitter, not over-styling.
- **Real plates, real spoons.** A worn wooden board reads warmer than studio porcelain.
- **Top-down or 3/4 angles** for hero shots. Eye-level for tabletop scenes.
- **Natural daylight** is the default. Warm tungsten allowed for evening/comfort moods.
- **Garnish honestly.** A torn herb, a crack of pepper, a finger smudge on a bowl edge. Flawesome over flawless.

#### Lifestyle Photography Principles

- **Hands over faces.** Cooking is what we do, not who we are. Lead with the cook's hands stirring, pouring, tasting.
- **Real kitchens.** A scuffed hob, a cluttered counter, a tea towel mid-fold. Aspirational interiors are off-brand.
- **Inclusive casting** — age, ethnicity, ability, family structure. Single cooks and shared meals both matter.
- **Quiet moments.** A pause to taste. A second-helping ladle. A lid lifted to release steam. The brand lives in pauses.

#### Composition & Emotion Rules

- Generous negative space. Let the dish breathe.
- Warmth in light, restraint in styling.
- Earned imperfection: a spill on the counter, a slightly off-centre ladle — only when it adds. Never staged mess.
- Avoid: cold studio aesthetics, over-saturated supermarket gloss, drone-shot tablescapes, anything that looks like a stock photo.

### 3.7 Iconography & Graphics

- **Simple, rounded, line-based.** Same DNA as the Pillow's soft asymmetry.
- 2 px stroke at 24 px size; scale proportionally.
- Outline icons for UI; filled icons only for category/variant markers on pack.
- Permitted illustrative motifs: steam wisps, ladle silhouettes, ingredient line drawings, simmer rings.
- Always supporting the photography and the Pillow — never competing.

### 3.8 Sonic Identity *(optional, in development)*

If sonic is rolled out, the brand mnemonic should evoke a slow simmer or the gentle clink of a wooden spoon on the side of a pot. Two- to three-note motif maximum. Audio sign-off after the tagline. Pure form maintained in Day-to-Day; reinterpretations allowed in Brand Moments with approval.

## 4. Product Presentation Guidelines

### 4.1 Styling Principles
- **Hero the finished dish first.** Stock cube on its own is rarely the headline.
- **Show the unwrap or the drop.** A cube hitting hot water, a stock pot peeling open, a swirl of bouillon dissolving — these are our hero product moments.
- **Show the source.** Where helpful, surround the product with the real ingredients it captures (carrot tops, leek greens, beef bones styled as ingredients, not props).

### 4.2 Realism vs Aspiration
Rnorr sits at **honest realism with quiet warmth**. The dish on the pack must be achievable by the cook holding the pack. We do not over-style, photoshop ingredients in, or imply chef-level outcomes from supermarket inputs.

### 4.3 Consistency Across Markets
- The Pillow and Rnorr Green appear identically on every pack worldwide.
- Pack architecture (logo placement, flavor band, dish photography) follows a single global template.
- Local cuisines are honoured in the dish photography and variant naming — a Thai stock pot's hero dish is tom yum, not consommé.
- Variant typography, layout, and brand voice remain globally consistent.

## 5. Messaging & Copywriting Framework

### 5.1 Messaging Pillars

Every campaign ladders up to one or more:

1. **Depth of flavor** — real taste, built from real ingredients.
2. **Time well kept** — what you save in effort goes back to the table.
3. **Confidence in the kitchen** — anyone can cook with depth.
4. **Real ingredients** — recognisable, transparent, honest.

### 5.2 Language Patterns
- **Short sentences.** Headlines under 8 words wherever possible.
- **Sensory specificity.** "Slow-simmered onion" beats "rich flavor."
- **Active, present tense.** "Stir it in. Done." not "You can stir it in to enjoy."
- **Kitchen vocabulary.** Real words real cooks use: simmer, deglaze, season, sweat, build, finish.
- **Numbers when honest.** "5 minutes, 1 cube, 4 servings." Numbers do work copy can't.

### 5.3 Emotional Triggers
- **Comfort** (a meal that tastes cared-for)
- **Pride** (you made this)
- **Belonging** (the family table)
- **Calm** (one less thing to figure out)
- **Generosity** (feeding others well)

### 5.4 Tagline Discipline
- "Tastes like time." is a closing line — TVC end cards, signature brand campaigns, master packaging.
- Not used as a closer on every social post or banner.
- Always set in Rubik Medium Italic with the period.
- Localised translations only when Legal-approved.

### 5.5 Localisation Guidelines
- **Translate the truth, not the words.** A "Tuesday pasta" insight becomes a "Wednesday rice" insight in a market where Wednesday is the weeknight pivot.
- **Lead with the local dish.** Hero imagery on local packs and campaigns shows the cuisine of the market.
- **Local talent, local cooking.** Hand and kitchen photography must reflect the market it appears in.
- **Pricing and pack sizes** localise; brand architecture does not.

## 6. Audience Segmentation

### 6.1 Everyday Feeders
- **Insight:** Cooking is non-negotiable, time isn't. A weeknight dinner has to happen, fast, for everyone at the table.
- **Hooks:** Stock cubes, bouillons, base-meal flavor builders. 15-minute recipes. Family-friendly variants.
- **Tone:** Practical, encouraging, no fuss. "Dinner, sorted."

### 6.2 Ambitious Home Cooks
- **Insight:** They love to cook, take it seriously, and want depth without spending all day on bones.
- **Hooks:** Stock pots, premium bone-broth variants, "elevate-from-scratch" recipe content.
- **Tone:** Confident, knowing, peer-to-peer. "The shortcut chefs actually use."

### 6.3 New Cooks
- **Insight:** Learning to cook. Afraid of bland. Need wins early.
- **Hooks:** Bouillons, instructional content, beginner-friendly recipes built around one Rnorr product.
- **Tone:** Warm, generous, never patronising. "Start here. You're closer than you think."

### 6.4 Soup-as-Meal Seekers
- **Insight:** Soup is dinner, lunch, comfort, and self-care. They want soups that feel real.
- **Hooks:** Soup range, broth-as-base concepts, single-serving solutions.
- **Tone:** Comforting, restorative, gently indulgent. "A real bowl, real fast."

### 6.5 Health- and Ingredient-Conscious Cooks
- **Insight:** Reads the back of the pack. Cares about salt, additives, sourcing.
- **Hooks:** Lower-salt variants, recognised-ingredient lines, transparency content.
- **Tone:** Honest, specific, non-defensive. "Here's exactly what's in it."

## 7. Campaign System Framework

### 7.1 Campaign Portfolio Framework

| Tier | Share | Role | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Always-on / Core** | 70% | Sustaining presence across core range. Recipe content, pack visibility, value comms, seasonal cooking. | Weeknight recipe drops, app deals, in-store POP, breakfast/lunch/dinner social. |
| **Brand Moments** | 20% | Tentpole campaigns that stretch identity, celebrate the brand, or own a cultural cooking moment. | Sunday Roast season, Soup Season, the Pillow's anniversary, Holiday Cooking. |
| **Partnerships / Chefs** | 10% | Co-created with chefs, food creators, or cultural collaborators. | Chef-collab recipe lines, creator-led variants, retailer-exclusive limited editions. |

### 7.2 Campaign Types

#### 7.2.1 Product Launch
- Hero the dish, then the new product.
- One Kitchen Truth as hero message.
- The variant earns its visual moment through dish hero and Rnorr Yellow accent — not through a new colour.
- TVC + social + recipe content + in-store + pack reveal — synchronised.

#### 7.2.2 Seasonal Cooking Moment
- Tied to a real cooking season (soup, BBQ, holiday roast, back-to-school weeknights).
- Always begins with the dish, not the product.
- Often a Brand Moment with treated Pillow latitude (with approval).

#### 7.2.3 Value Campaign
- Bold Antonio typography on White or Rnorr Green.
- Clear price, clear pack, clear meal outcome.
- Rnorr Yellow for the price callout. Rnorr Green for the brand anchor.
- Copy: ≤6 words headline, single CTA.

#### 7.2.4 Brand Campaign
- Long-form storytelling. Permission for warmth, humour, and quiet emotion.
- Hero is always the cook and the meal. Pack appears only as a witness.
- Tagline used at sign-off — earned, not pasted.

#### 7.2.5 Partnership / Chef Collaboration
- Co-created identity within Rnorr's rules. The Pillow stays Rnorr Green.
- Partner's voice and Rnorr's voice intersect; Rnorr is the host, not the louder voice.
- Always recipe-led: the collaboration's reason for existing is the dish.

### 7.3 Campaign Components

Every campaign has:

1. **Hero Message** — one Kitchen Truth or product truth, max 8 words.
2. **Supporting Message** — 1–2 lines proving or extending the hero, no more.
3. **Visual System** — dish-led hero photography + Pillow brand anchor + (if applicable) Rnorr Yellow accent for appetite or value.
4. **CTA Pattern** — verb-led, ≤3 words: "Try the Recipe" / "Find In-Store" / "Add to Basket" / "Save the Recipe."
5. **Sign-off** — Pillow + (optional) tagline.

### 7.4 Channel Strategy

| Channel | Role | Brand Vol | Product Vol | Key Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Social (organic)** | Recipe-led community, Kitchen Truths, light humour | Med | Med | Friend-in-the-kitchen voice. Recipe = the asset. |
| **Social (paid)** | Conversion, recipe download, reach | High | High | Single offer, dish hero, clear CTA. |
| **TV / OLV** | Emotional storytelling, brand moments | Med | Med | End card: Pillow + tagline. Five-second sign-off discipline. |
| **In-store / POP** | Decision at shelf | High | High | Pack hero, Rnorr Yellow price flag, clean shelf disruption. |
| **Mobile / App / Recipe Hub** | Recipe-led utility, loyalty | Low | High | Microcopy clarity, save/share-friendly. |
| **OOH** | Brand fame, dish hunger | Very High | Med | ≤6-word headline, dish hero, Pillow anchor. |
| **Packaging** | Last-meter brand voice | High | Very High | Pillow front-and-centre. Dish honest. Back-of-pack: real ingredients, real cooking instructions. |
| **Email / CRM** | Recipe drops, seasonal cooking | Med | High | Subject ≤45 chars. Recipe-card preview. Single CTA. |

### 7.5 Content Templates

#### 7.5.1 Recipe-Led Instagram Post
- **Visual:** Top-down or 3/4 dish hero. Pillow bottom-right corner.
- **Caption:** Headline (Kitchen Truth, ≤8 words) + recipe outcome (1 line) + ingredient hook + CTA ("Save for Tuesday").
- **Format:** Carousel preferred — slide 1 dish, slide 2 ingredients laid flat, slide 3 method steps, slide 4 finished plate.

#### 7.5.2 Video Ad Storyboard (20s)
- **0–3s:** Dish close-up — steam, drip, gloss. Sound: ambient kitchen.
- **3–10s:** A cook's hands at work. One Kitchen Truth voiced or supered.
- **10–15s:** The Rnorr moment — a cube drops, a stock pot peels, a bouillon dissolves.
- **15–18s:** Finished dish on table, served.
- **18–20s:** Pillow + tagline ("Tastes like time.") + sonic sign-off.

#### 7.5.3 Banner Ad (Static)
- **Layout:** Dish hero left, Pillow top-right, headline + CTA right.
- **Colour:** White base, Rnorr Green accent, Rnorr Yellow for price (if value).
- **Headline:** ≤6 words. CTA: ≤3 words. No tagline.

#### 7.5.4 OOH / Billboard
- **Asset:** Dish hero filling the canvas, or Pillow at scale on Rnorr Green.
- **Headline:** ≤5 words. Sensory or Kitchen Truth.
- **No CTA.** Brand recall is the CTA.

#### 7.5.5 In-app Push Notification
- Subject ≤30 chars ("Tonight's dinner, sorted."). Body ≤90 chars. One CTA verb. No tagline.

#### 7.5.6 Email
- Subject ≤45 chars. Preheader ≤90 chars. Recipe-card hero. Single primary CTA ("See the Recipe"). Pillow in footer.

#### 7.5.7 Packaging (Master Architecture)
- **Front:** Pillow top-centre. Variant name in Antonio Bold below Pillow. Dish hero filling the lower two-thirds. Rnorr Yellow accent band along the bottom edge (where appetite cue is needed).
- **Back:** Real-ingredient callout, recipe suggestion, cooking instructions in Rubik Regular, allergens and legal in Rubik Regular at 7–8 pt.
- **Side:** "Tastes like time." tagline + minimal nutrition strip.

## 8. Digital & UX Guidelines

### 8.1 App & Website Tone
- **Recipe-led utility first**, warmth second. Get the cook to the dish quickly, then add the friend-in-the-kitchen touch.
- Error states stay human: "Couldn't find that recipe — try a different ingredient?" — not "Search returned no results."
- Confirmations specific: "Recipe saved. We'll remind you Tuesday."

### 8.2 UI Consistency Principles
- White as primary surface; Rnorr Green for primary actions, wayfinding, brand moments.
- Rnorr Yellow for highlights, badges, price flags, and reward states (CTAs stay Rnorr Green).
- Charcoal type on White for body throughout digital.
- Pillow used as a UI module for recipe cards, tip callouts, and saved-item containers.
- Antonio for display type; Rubik for body, UI, and microcopy. No system font fallbacks in production-quality builds.

### 8.3 Microcopy Guidelines
- **CTAs:** verb-led, ≤3 words. "See Recipe," "Add to Cart," "Find Stockists," "Save for Later."
- **Empty states:** warm, specific. "No saved recipes yet — start with a Tuesday classic →"
- **Confirmations:** human and specific. "Onion stock pot added to your basket."
- **Loading:** brief. A brand wink permitted only at long waits (>2s): "Simmering…"

### 8.4 Accessibility
- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum.
- Alt text on every dish image — descriptive ("a bowl of golden chicken broth with parsley") not promotional.
- Recipe steps usable by screen readers, with structured numbered lists.
- Don't rely on flavor-accent colour alone to identify variant — always pair with name.
- Audio described where TVC is published with subtitles.

## 10. Compliance & Brand Safety

### 10.1 Legal Approvals
- **All advertising materials** require clearance by local Rnorr country counsel or Global Marketing/IP Legal before publication.
- **Treated Pillow or wordmark** uses require Global Brand + Legal IP approval at concept and pre-final stages.
- **New trademarks, slogans, or variant names** require Legal review before any external use.

### 10.2 Fonts & Licensing
- Antonio and Rubik (both Open Font License) are the licensed Rnorr typefaces, free for commercial use across all applications.
- Any non-Antonio / non-Rubik usage requires a verified commercial-use licence covering the application (digital, print, broadcast, app, packaging) and Legal sign-off.

### 10.3 Food Claims & Imagery
- **Imagery must reflect the actual product** the consumer receives. No swapped-in higher-grade ingredients, no exaggerated portion sizes, no impossible plating.
- **Regulated claims** ("natural," "real ingredients," "no added MSG," "reduced salt," "made with real chicken") must be substantiated per market regulation. Always verify with Regulatory before publication.
- **Nutritional and allergen labelling** follow local regulation; never abbreviated.
- **Sustainability and sourcing claims** require documented supply-chain verification. Vague claims ("sustainable," "natural") without specifics are off-brand and high-risk legally.

### 10.4 Cultural Sensitivity
- Casting, kitchen styling, and dish selection must reflect the market the asset appears in.
- Avoid stereotypes of any cuisine, region, or cook archetype.
- Religious and dietary sensitivities (halal, kosher, vegetarian, vegan) are honoured on-pack and in content where products qualify.
- Humour brings the audience in. Humour at the expense of a cuisine, a skill level, or a market is off-brand.

### 10.5 Confidentiality
Internal use only across the Rnorr System: employees, franchisees, agencies, suppliers, distributors, retail partners. Not for external distribution without Legal approval.

### 11.2 Campaign Generation Rules

Any generated campaign **must**:

1. **Map to one portfolio tier** — Always-on / Brand Moment / Partnership (70/20/10).
2. **Hero the finished dish, not the pack.** Pack is a witness, not the headline.
3. **Anchor on one Kitchen Truth** — real, shared, resolved by Rnorr.
4. **Apply Rnorr Green and the Pillow** as the brand anchor on every asset.
5. **Use colour proportion** appropriate to engagement length:
   - Glance (≤3s): brand 10, product 1
   - Browse (1–2 min): brand 6, product 5
   - Cook (2+ min): brand 2, product 10
6. **Use Antonio for display, Rubik for body** in approved weights, headlines ≤8 words.
7. **Hold the three-colour discipline** — Rnorr Green, Rnorr Yellow, White only. No extended palette unless explicitly approved.
8. **Tagline reserved for sign-off.** Not used in workhorse comms.
9. **CTA verb-led, ≤3 words.**
10. **Pass the friend-in-the-kitchen voice test.**
11. **No forbidden visuals** — no studio gloss, no drone tablescapes, no chef-snob staging.
12. **No regulated claims** ("natural," "real ingredients," "no MSG," etc.) without Regulatory clearance.

### 11.3 Generation Workflow (Agent Sequence)

```
1. CLASSIFY input → {tier, campaign_type, audience, channel, market, product_variant}
2. RETRIEVE Kitchen Truth aligned to audience + cooking occasion
3. RETRIEVE local hero dish for the market (if localised)

5. DRAFT supporting message (1–2 lines)
6. SELECT visual system:
     - color_proportion based on engagement length
     - asset_type: dish_hero | pillow_anchor | recipe_card | variant_spotlight
     - yellow_accent if value-led or appetite-led moment
7. APPLY typography rules + colour pairing matrix
8. ASSEMBLE per channel template (Section 7.5)
9. VALIDATE against 11.1 + 11.2 → flag every violation
10. ROUTE for human + Legal review where:
     - treated Pillow appears
     - regulated claim appears
     - new partnership or IP appears
     - new market-first localisation appears
```
