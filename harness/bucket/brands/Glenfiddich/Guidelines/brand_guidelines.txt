# Glenfiddich × Aston Martin F1® Team
## Partnership Brand Guidelines

**Co-Brand Identity & Activation System**
*Version 1.0 — Built for partnership marketing teams, both parent brands' agencies, and AI-driven campaign generation systems.*

> **Partnership context.** A premium collaboration uniting Glenfiddich's whisky-making heritage with the Aston Martin Aramco Formula One® Team's engineering and racing performance — to create a new standard of luxury experience. Craft meets performance. Heritage meets innovation.

> **Document purpose.** A single source of truth for co-branded work. This governs only the *partnership* expression. It does not replace either parent brand's own guidelines.

---

## 0. Governance & Precedence (READ FIRST)

This is a **co-brand guideline**, not a standalone brand. Three rules govern everything below:

1. **Parent guidelines outrank this document.** Where this guideline conflicts with either the Glenfiddich Global Brand Guidelines or the Aston Martin F1® Team Brand Guidelines, the relevant parent guideline wins. This document only covers how the two come *together*.
2. **Dual approval is mandatory.** Every co-branded asset requires sign-off from **both** Glenfiddich Global Brand and Aston Martin F1® Team Brand, plus both legal teams, before production and before publication. No exceptions.
3. **Alcohol-marketing compliance is absolute.** Glenfiddich is an alcohol brand. Every asset, in every market, must comply with that market's alcohol marketing code and with the firewall rules in Section 10. This overrides creative ambition, partner enthusiasm, and campaign deadlines.

If any of the three above is uncertain for a given piece of work, the work does not proceed until resolved.

## 1. Partnership Overview

### 1.1 Core Promise
**Blending craftsmanship with high-performance innovation.**

The test every co-branded asset must pass: does this authentically express *both* whisky craft *and* racing performance — neither subordinated, neither decorative?

### 1.2 The Shared Philosophy
Two iconic houses, one belief: that excellence is earned through precision, patience, and the relentless pursuit of better. Glenfiddich has pursued it in oak and copper since 1887. Aston Martin pursues it in carbon and milliseconds. The partnership exists where those two disciplines meet.

### 1.3 What the Partnership Celebrates
- **Glenfiddich's** whisky-making heritage — maturation, mastery, the Malt Master's craft.
- **Aston Martin's** engineering and racing performance — aerodynamics, innovation, the pursuit of pace.
- Together: **honour the past, shape the present, forge the future.**

### 1.4 Operational Promise
Three commitments:
1. **Exceptional quality through mastery** — both houses refuse compromise.
2. **Craft, collaboration, and performance** celebrated as one story.
3. **Premium experiences beyond the product** — releases, events, and access money alone can't buy.

### 1.5 One-Line Positioning
*A premium collaboration uniting whisky craftsmanship and Formula One® performance to create a new standard of luxury experience.*

### 1.6 Partnership Scope
- Limited-edition whiskies (e.g., aged collections released around the partnership).
- Race-week activations and global events.
- Travel-retail exclusives.
- Immersive brand storytelling across touchpoints.

## 2. Personality & Voice

### 2.1 Partnership Archetype
**Creator** (mastery, making, craft) meeting **Ruler** (excellence, prestige, standard-setting). The partnership doesn't chase trends or shout. It sets the benchmark and lets the work speak.

### 2.2 Personality Attributes
- **Precision-led** — meticulous in whisky and in racing.
- **High-performance** — speed, innovation, evolution.
- **Heritage-driven** — legacy, authenticity, craft.
- **Progressive** — future-focused.
- **Exclusive** — limited, premium, earned.

### 2.3 Tone of Voice
- **Sophisticated and confident** — assured, never boastful.
- **Understated yet powerful** — restraint is the flex.
- **Emotionally deep** — premium storytelling, not feature lists.

### 2.4 Voice Principle
**Let the craft speak. Add nothing it doesn't need.**

Both houses earn attention through the work, not the volume. Copy is spare, precise, and confident. If a line is doing decoration rather than meaning, cut it.

### 2.5 Creative Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Two crafts, one standard** | Every asset must hold both worlds in balance — the still house and the pit lane, the cask and the chassis. Never let one become wallpaper for the other. |
| **Precision is the aesthetic** | Detail is the brand. The grain of oak, the weave of carbon, the exact pour, the apex of a corner. We show mastery through specificity. |
| **Restraint is luxury** | Negative space, few words, considered motion. The premium signal is what we leave out. |

### 2.6 Do's and Don'ts of Communication

**DO**
- Hold both crafts in balance — whisky and racing, equally honoured.
- Lead with detail and mastery.
- Keep copy spare, confident, precise.
- Let cinematic imagery carry the emotion.
- Reference the shared values (precision, patience, performance) as the connective tissue.

**DON'T**
- Make either brand decorative or subordinate.
- Use hype language ("insane," "epic," "game-changing"). This is understated luxury.
- Imply or depict alcohol consumption in connection with driving, racing, or vehicle operation (see Section 10 — this is a hard firewall).
- Over-explain the partnership. Let it be felt.
- Use motorsport adrenaline to sell the act of drinking.

### 2.7 Example Messaging
- "Where craft meets performance."
- "Engineered with precision. Crafted with passion."
- "A new standard, shaped together."
- "Mastery, in oak and in carbon."
- "Two houses. One pursuit."
- **Signature line:** "Where craft meets performance." — used as the partnership sign-off, sparingly.

## 3. Visual Identity System

### 3.1 The Co-Brand Lockup

The partnership mark is a **dual lockup** of the Aston Martin F1® Team wings and the Glenfiddich stag, in two configurations.

#### Horizontal Lockup
Aston Martin F1® Team wordmark/wings on the **left**, a thin vertical divider rule, Glenfiddich stag + wordmark on the **right**. Used for wide formats — website headers, OOH, banners, event backdrops.

#### Vertical Lockup
Glenfiddich stag + wordmark **on top**, a horizontal divider rule, Aston Martin F1® Team wings + wordmark **below**. Used for portrait and square formats — social, packaging, posters, vertical signage.

#### The Divider Rule
A single thin rule (vertical in the horizontal lockup, horizontal in the vertical lockup) separates and equalises the two marks. The rule is part of the lockup — it signals partnership-of-equals. It is rendered in the same colour as the marks (white, lime, or dark depending on the version).

#### Lockup Construction Rules (non-negotiable)
1. **Both marks always at balanced optical weight.** Neither brand dominates. The lockup is a partnership of equals.
2. **The divider rule is always present** between the two marks.
3. **Approved lockup files only.** Never recreate, redraw, or re-space the lockup by hand. Use the provided artwork.
4. **Both marks always in the same colourway** — both white, both lime, or both dark. Never mix (e.g., never lime stag with white wings).

#### Clear Space
- Clearspace equals **the height of the Glenfiddich wordmark cap-height**, applied on all sides of the full lockup.
- Nothing — type, image edge, other logos — enters the clear space.

#### Minimum Size
- Horizontal lockup: **Print 40 mm wide** / **Digital 240 px wide**.
- Vertical lockup: **Print 30 mm wide** / **Digital 180 px wide**.
- Below minimum, the lockup loses legibility (the stag detail and wing lines collapse). Use a single parent mark per approval instead, or scale the layout up.

#### Trademark
- "Formula One," "F1," and related marks are trademarks of Formula One Licensing BV — always carry the ™ as shown in the approved lockup.
- The Aston Martin wings and Glenfiddich stag carry their own ® / ™ as specified in each parent's guidelines.
- Never alter, remove, or reposition any trademark symbol in the lockup.

#### DO
- Use the approved horizontal or vertical lockup as supplied.
- Keep both marks in a single colourway.
- Give the lockup generous clear space — restraint is the brand.
- Choose the lockup orientation that fits the format cleanly.

#### DO NOT
- Resize one mark relative to the other.
- Remove or restyle the divider rule.
- Mix colourways between the two marks.
- Recreate the lockup in live text or redraw either emblem.
- Apply effects (shadow, gradient, glow, bevel) to the lockup.
- Rotate, skew, or distort the lockup.
- Place the lockup on a busy background that compromises either mark.
- Lock either partner mark up with a *third* party's logo without dual approval + that third party's permission.

### 3.2 Parent Mark Integrity

Within the lockup, **each emblem must comply with its own parent brand guidelines**:
- The **Glenfiddich stag** — its proportions, the "1887" date, the "GLENFIDDICH" banner detail, and the wordmark are fixed by Glenfiddich. Never simplify or redraw the stag.
- The **Aston Martin F1® Team wings** — the wing-line count, the "ASTON MARTIN" bar, and the "FORMULA ONE™ TEAM" wordmark are fixed by Aston Martin F1. Never alter the wing geometry.

If a parent updates its mark, the lockup is reissued centrally. Teams never modify either emblem locally.

### 3.3 Signature Line — "Where craft meets performance."

The partnership's verbal sign-off.

- **Use with discipline.** A sign-off, not a tagline for every asset. Overuse cheapens it.
- **Recommended use:** film end cards, hero campaign moments, premium print, event headlines.
- **Set in:** Aston Martin Flare Medium, sentence case.
- **Colour:** Lime (#C4F966) or White on dark/green grounds; Racing Green (#15433C) on light grounds.
- **Clearspace:** equal to the cap-height of the typeset line.
- **Localisation:** market-approved translation only; English retention permitted in premium global contexts. Confirm with both legal teams.

### 3.4 Colour Palette

The partnership palette is **dark, premium, and restrained** — racing green as the foundation, lime as the single high-energy accent, off-white for space.

#### Master Palette

| Colour | Role | RGB | HEX |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Racing Green** | Primary ground — the partnership's signature surface | 21.67.60 | **#15433C** |
| **Lime** | The single accent — energy, performance, highlight | 196.249.102 | **#C4F966** |
| **Off-White** | Breathing-room base, type on dark grounds | 249.249.249 | **#F9F9F9** |

- **Racing Green** is the foundation. It nods to Aston Martin's British Racing Green heritage and to Glenfiddich's bottle-and-glen world. It is the default background for premium partnership work.
- **Lime** is the only high-energy colour in the system. It signals performance and modernity. Used sparingly — a single accent, never a field. Lime is what stops the palette feeling staid.
- **Off-White** is the canvas and the primary type colour on dark grounds. Never pure #FFFFFF in large fields (reserved as secondary).

#### Secondary Palette

| Colour | RGB | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **White** | 255.255.255 | **#FFFFFF** | Lockup white version, type, fine detail on dark |
| **Black** | 0.0.0 | **#000000** | Lockup dark version ground, premium black contexts, legal type |

#### Signature Gradient

| Gradient | From | To | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Depth Gradient** | Racing Green #15433C | Deep Green #0D1E1B | Backgrounds, film grounds, premium surfaces. Adds cinematic depth. Always dark-to-darker; direction top-to-bottom or radial-from-centre. |

The Depth Gradient is the partnership's premium signature surface. It is used for full-bleed backgrounds in film, digital, and print. It must remain subtle — a deepening, not a spotlight. Never invert (never light-to-dark in a way that creates a glow), never introduce a third colour into the gradient.

#### Functional Palette

| Colour | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Charcoal** | #1A1A1A | Body type on Off-White surfaces |
| **Mid Gray** | #6E6E6E | Secondary type, captions, dividers |

#### Colour Proportion Rules
- **Racing Green dominant** — the default ground for premium work.
- **Off-White / White** for type and breathing room on dark grounds.
- **Lime as a single accent** — one lime element per composition is the discipline. Two is the maximum. Lime as a full background is off-brand except for short motion moments (a transition flash, a bumper).
- Generous negative space. Restraint is the luxury signal.

#### Colour DO / DO NOT
- **DO** build premium work on Racing Green or the Depth Gradient.
- **DO** use Lime as the single performance accent.
- **DO** use Off-White for type and space on dark grounds.
- **DO** use the black lockup version on light/metallic premium contexts.
- **DO NOT** use Lime as a large background field in static design.
- **DO NOT** introduce metallic gold, silver, or any colour outside the palette without dual brand approval (the brief mentions "metallic accents" — these require approved foil/spot specs from both brands, not invented colours).
- **DO NOT** add a third colour to the Depth Gradient.
- **DO NOT** place Lime type on Off-White (fails contrast — see accessibility).

#### Accessibility
- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum for all body copy: 4.5:1.
- **Off-White on Racing Green** — passes AAA. Primary pairing for premium dark layouts.
- **White on Racing Green** — passes AAA.
- **Lime on Racing Green** — passes AA for large text (18 px+); use for headlines and accents, not body.
- **Lime on Black** — passes AAA.
- **Charcoal on Off-White** — passes AAA. Primary body pairing for light layouts.
- **Racing Green on Off-White** — passes AAA. Approved for headlines and body on light grounds.
- **Lime on Off-White / White** — **fails contrast.** Never use Lime as type on light grounds.
- Never rely on colour alone to carry meaning.

### 3.5 Typography

Two typefaces, clear division of labour. **Aston Martin Flare** is the partnership's display voice (it carries the racing house's identity); **Agrandir** is the secondary and body voice.

#### Aston Martin Flare — Display
The primary partnership typeface for headlines, hero callouts, the signature line, and premium display. Its character carries the Aston Martin identity into every partnership headline.

- **Aston Martin Flare Bold** — hero headlines, OOH, primary display.
- **Aston Martin Flare Medium** — subheads, the signature line, secondary display, premium captions.
- **Aston Martin Flare Regular** — large supporting type, pull quotes.
- **Use for type 20 px / 12 pt and larger.** Not a body face.

#### Agrandir — Secondary & Body
A clean grotesque for body, captions, UI, specs, and editorial. Carries information clearly while staying refined.

- **Agrandir Regular** — body copy, captions, specs, ingredient/maturation detail, UI, legal-adjacent copy.

(Only Agrandir Regular is supplied. Where additional emphasis is needed in body, use Racing Green or Lime colour, or Aston Martin Flare for the emphasised element — do not fake-bold Agrandir.)

System fallback (where brand fonts can't be embedded): Times/serif display fallback is **not** appropriate — fall back to a clean grotesque (Helvetica Neue → Arial) for both display and body rather than mismatch the Flare character. Production should always embed the licensed fonts.

#### Type Roles

| Element | Font | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | Aston Martin Flare | Bold |
| Subhead / signature line | Aston Martin Flare | Medium |
| Pull quote / large support | Aston Martin Flare | Regular |
| Body / caption / specs | Agrandir | Regular |
| UI label / button | Agrandir | Regular, tracked +25, UPPERCASE |
| Legal / fine print | Agrandir | Regular at 7–8 pt |

#### Typesetting Rules
- **Headlines:** max 8 words. Sentence case for storytelling; UPPERCASE permitted for short, declarative lines (≤4 words) to echo the lockup wordmarks.
- **Subheads:** Aston Martin Flare Medium, 1.2× leading.
- **Body:** Agrandir Regular, min 16 px digital / 9 pt print, 1.5× leading.
- **Emphasis:** colour (Racing Green or Lime) or a switch to Flare — never fake-bold.
- **Tracking:** Flare at display sizes (40 pt+) benefits from slightly tight tracking (−10 to −20); Agrandir body at default; UI labels tracked +25.
- **Restraint:** few words, much space. The typographic signal of luxury is air.

#### Print Type/Colour Pairings (DO)

| Surface | Type colour |
|---|---|
| Racing Green / Depth Gradient | Off-White, White, or Lime (headlines/accents only) |
| Off-White | Charcoal, Racing Green |
| Black | Off-White, White, or Lime |
| Light photography | Charcoal or Racing Green |
| Dark photography | Off-White or White |

#### Digital Type/Colour Pairings (DO)

| Surface | Type colour |
|---|---|
| Racing Green | Off-White (body), Lime (headline/accent) |
| Off-White | Charcoal (body), Racing Green (headline) |
| Black | Off-White, Lime |
| Photography (light) | Charcoal |
| Photography (dark) | Off-White |

#### Forbidden in digital body copy
- Aston Martin Flare for body (display only).
- Lime type on Off-White or White (insufficient contrast).
- Lime type at body sizes under 18 px on any ground.
- Fake-bolding Agrandir via CSS.
- All-caps body copy (reserved for short headlines and UI).

#### Licensing
Aston Martin Flare and Agrandir are licensed for partnership use through the respective rights-holders. **Confirm the licence scope with both legal teams before any new application** (broadcast, OOH, packaging, digital, third-party co-marketing) — font licences for partnership use may be narrower than each parent's internal licence. Never substitute an unlicensed lookalike.

### 3.6 Imagery & Film

Cinematic, premium, detail-obsessed. Two worlds — the still house and the circuit — shot with the same reverence for craft.

#### The Two Worlds

**Whisky craft (Glenfiddich world):**
- The Malt Master's hands, the copper stills, the oak, the warehouse, the pour, the colour of the liquid in glass.
- Warm, low, directional light. Amber, brass, deep green, shadow.
- Stillness, patience, time. The opposite of speed — and that contrast is the point.

**Racing performance (Aston Martin F1 world):**
- The car, the carbon weave, the pit lane, the engineering bay, the wheel, the apex, motion.
- Cool, precise, high-contrast light. Green, carbon black, metallic, lime flashes.
- Speed, focus, milliseconds.

#### Composition & Emotion Rules
- **Detail over wide.** The grain of oak, the weave of carbon, the meniscus of a pour, the texture of a tyre. Macro tells the craft story.
- **Motion for the car, stillness for the whisky.** Use the contrast deliberately — a long, still pour cut against a fast, precise lap.
- **Cinematic grade.** Deep greens, controlled contrast, restrained colour. Never over-saturated, never flat.
- **Premium restraint.** Few elements, much space, considered framing. Luxury is what's left out.
- Avoid: lifestyle "party" imagery, crowds with drinks, anything that reads as mass-market or promotional-discount.

#### CRITICAL — Alcohol & Vehicle Separation in Imagery
This is a hard firewall, not a guideline (full rules in Section 10):
- **Never** show whisky (or any alcohol) inside, on, or adjacent to the race car, in the pit lane during running, with a driver in race context, or anywhere that associates consumption with driving or vehicle operation.
- **Never** depict a driver, or anyone in racewear, holding or consuming alcohol.
- **Never** intercut a pour with a car in motion in a way that links the two acts.
- Whisky imagery and on-track imagery live in **separate frames, separate moments, separate contexts.** The partnership is craft-meets-craft, never drink-meets-drive.

### 3.7 Iconography & Graphics
- Fine-line, precise, engineered geometry — echoing both the wing-lines of the Aston badge and the etched detail of the Glenfiddich stag.
- 1.5–2 px hairline strokes; precise corners.
- Permitted motifs: contour/elevation lines, racing-line curves, cask-end circles, fine measurement marks.
- Lime used only as a single accent within any graphic.
- Always supporting the lockup and imagery — never decorative clutter.

### 3.8 Motion & Sonic
- Motion is precise and weighted — easing that feels engineered, never bouncy or playful.
- The Depth Gradient and Lime accents can animate (a lime line tracing a racing curve, a slow reveal of the lockup).
- Any sonic identity must be jointly approved; neither parent's audio brand may be used in partnership context without that parent's sign-off.

## 5. Messaging & Copywriting Framework

### 5.1 Messaging Pillars
1. **Craft & Mastery** — the made thing, perfected over time.
2. **Performance & Precision** — speed, engineering, the margin of milliseconds.
3. **Heritage & Future** — honouring the past, forging what's next.
4. **Exclusive Experience** — access, rarity, the premium beyond the product.

### 5.2 Language Patterns
- **Spare and precise.** Short, declarative, confident.
- **Specific over superlative.** "21 years in oak" beats "exceptionally aged." "Eight-tenths through Eau Rouge" beats "incredibly fast."
- **Shared-value connective tissue.** Precision, patience, performance — the words that belong to both houses.
- **Restraint.** Never hype. Never exclamation. The confidence is in the calm.

### 5.3 Emotional Register
- **Reverence** (for craft, for time, for mastery)
- **Anticipation** (the release, the race, the reveal)
- **Belonging** (the few who get access)
- **Pride** (in two houses at the top of their game)

### 5.4 Signature-Line Discipline
- "Where craft meets performance." closes hero films and premium moments — not every asset.
- Set in Aston Martin Flare Medium.
- Localised translations only when both legal teams approve.

### 5.5 Localisation
- Translate intent, not words; preserve the understated register.
- Respect each market's alcohol-marketing code in copy (no consumption encouragement, no strength-as-benefit claims, etc. — Section 10).
- Age-gate every market touchpoint per local law.

## 6. Audience

The partnership speaks to a **premium, experience-driven, of-legal-drinking-age** audience.

### 6.1 Luxury Consumers & Collectors
- **Insight:** Value rarity, provenance, and craft story. Buy the edition and the experience.
- **Hooks:** Limited releases, cask provenance, design detail, collectibility.
- **Tone:** Reverent, precise, provenance-led.

### 6.2 Motorsport Enthusiasts (of legal drinking age)
- **Insight:** Follow the team, understand the engineering, crave access.
- **Hooks:** Race-week activations, behind-the-engineering content, team moments.
- **Tone:** Knowledgeable, performance-fluent, insider.

### 6.3 High-Income Experience Seekers
- **Insight:** Spend on experiences over objects. Want the access others can't get.
- **Hooks:** Events, hospitality, immersive storytelling, travel-retail exclusives.
- **Tone:** Exclusive, considered, invitation-led.

### 6.4 Brand Loyalists (both houses)
- **Insight:** Already devoted to Glenfiddich or Aston Martin; the crossover is the draw.
- **Hooks:** The story of two houses meeting, shared-values narrative, dual heritage.
- **Tone:** Insider, celebratory of both legacies.

> **Audience hard rule:** all audience targeting must exclude anyone below legal drinking age in-market, and must meet the platform/market minimum-age and audience-composition thresholds for alcohol advertising (Section 10).

- CTAs restrained and access-led; never urgency-driven, never consumption-encouraging.

### 8.3 UI
- Racing Green / Depth Gradient surfaces; Off-White type; Lime as single interactive accent (links, focus states).

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum.

- Captions on all film; descriptive alt text on imagery.

## 9. Global vs Local Adaptation

### 9.1 Globally Locked (never changes)
- The co-brand lockup construction (both configurations, divider rule, equal weight, single colourway).
- Racing Green (#15433C) as the partnership ground.
- The Depth Gradient.
- Aston Martin Flare (display) + Agrandir (body).
- The core promise and one-line positioning.
- The voice principle ("Let the craft speak").
- Both parents' mark integrity.
- The alcohol-and-vehicle firewall (Section 10).

### 9.2 Localisable
- Signature-line translation (dual-legal-approved).
- Talent and casting (of legal drinking age, market-appropriate).
- Market-specific responsible-drinking messaging and age thresholds.
- Local event and travel-retail specifics.
- Product availability and edition naming per market.

### 9.3 Regional Reality
- **Alcohol marketing law varies enormously by market** — some markets restrict or ban alcohol advertising entirely, restrict motorsport-alcohol association specifically, or impose strict media-placement and audience-composition rules. The partnership expression must be re-cleared per market; a creative approved in one market is **not** automatically approved in another.

## 10. Compliance & Brand Safety

> This section is not optional guidance. It is the operating licence for the partnership.

### 10.1 Dual Approval
- Every co-branded asset requires sign-off from **Glenfiddich Global Brand + Aston Martin F1® Team Brand + both legal teams** at concept and pre-final.
- Neither party publishes co-branded work unilaterally.

### 10.2 Alcohol Marketing — Hard Rules
Across all markets, co-branded alcohol communications must:
- **Age-gate** every owned touchpoint and target only audiences verified/composed above legal drinking age, meeting each platform's and market's alcohol-ad thresholds (commonly ≥legal drinking age and ≥a high % of of-age audience).
- **Never target, appeal to, or feature** people under legal drinking age. No talent who is, or appears to be, under 25 in alcohol-consumption contexts.
- **Never encourage excessive or irresponsible consumption,** present alcohol as a route to success/status/performance, or imply it enhances ability.
- **Carry responsible-drinking messaging** where required by market/code.
- **Comply with the strictest applicable code** when a piece runs across markets.

### 10.3 The Alcohol–Vehicle Firewall (CRITICAL)
Because this partnership pairs an alcohol brand with a motorsport team, the single highest-risk area is any association between drinking and driving. The firewall:
- **Never** depict, imply, or juxtapose alcohol consumption with driving, racing, being in/near the car, the pit lane in operation, or anyone in racewear.
- **Never** show a driver or team member holding, near, or consuming alcohol.
- **Never** edit a pour and a moving car together so the two acts read as connected.
- **Never** use racing adrenaline or speed as a reason/benefit to drink.
- Whisky content and on-track content remain in **separate frames, posts, and moments.** The link between the houses is *shared craft and values* — explicitly never *drink-and-drive*.
- Any asset that risks blurring this line is killed at concept, not fixed in edit.

### 10.4 Trademarks & IP
- "Formula One," "F1," "FIA Formula One World Championship" and related marks are trademarks of Formula One Licensing BV — use only as supplied, with ™, and only with F1 rights clearance.
- Aston Martin wings/wordmark and Glenfiddich stag/wordmark each remain the property of their owners; use only per each parent's guidelines and the approved lockup.
- No partnership asset may imply endorsement beyond the agreed partnership scope.

### 10.5 Product & Provenance Claims
- Age statements, cask types, ABV, awards, and maturation claims must be accurate and substantiated, and cleared by Glenfiddich.
- Performance/engineering claims about the team or car must be accurate and cleared by Aston Martin F1.

### 10.6 Cultural & Market Safety
- Respect each market's cultural and religious context around alcohol; some markets require alcohol-free partnership expressions or prohibit the association entirely.
- Casting reflects the premium, of-age, diverse global audience without stereotype.

### 10.7 Confidentiality
For internal use of the partnership system: both companies' employees, agencies, and approved suppliers. Not for external distribution without dual legal approval.

### 11.2 Campaign Generation Rules

Any generated co-branded concept **must**:

1. **Hold both crafts as equals** — whisky and racing, neither decorative.

8. **Respect both parents' mark integrity** — never redraw the stag or the wings.
9. **ENFORCE the alcohol–vehicle firewall** — never link, juxtapose, or imply alcohol with driving/racing/car/driver. Whisky and track stay in separate frames.
10. **ENFORCE alcohol marketing compliance** — age-gating, of-age audiences only, no consumption encouragement, responsible-drinking messaging where required.
11. **Carry trademarks correctly** — F1™, parent ® / ™ as supplied.
12. **Route to human + dual-legal review** for: any imagery decision near the firewall, any market-specific alcohol-law question, any new lockup treatment, any product/performance claim.
